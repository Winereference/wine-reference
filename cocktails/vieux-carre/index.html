Vieux Carré

Brandy · Stirred

Deep and spicy: brandy, rye, vermouth and herbs.

BaseCognac and rye
MethodStirred
GlassRocks glass
GarnishLemon twist or cherry

Ingredients

  • 30 ml cognac
  • 30 ml rye whiskey
  • 30 ml sweet red vermouth
  • 5 ml Bénédictine
  • 2 dashes Peychaud's bitters
  • 2 dashes Angostura bitters

How to make it

Stir with ice and strain over fresh ice.

History

Created by Walter Bergeron at the Hotel Monteleone in New Orleans in the 1930s; the name means the French Quarter.
