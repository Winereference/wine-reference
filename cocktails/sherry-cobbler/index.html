Sherry Cobbler

Wine & aperitif · Shaken

Nutty sherry, orange and crushed ice: light and refreshing.

BaseAmontillado or fino sherry
MethodShaken
GlassHighball or goblet with crushed ice
GarnishBerries, orange slice and mint

Ingredients

  • 90 ml amontillado or fino sherry
  • 10 ml sugar syrup
  • 2 orange slices

How to make it

Shake the sherry, syrup and orange slices with ice and strain over crushed ice.

History

One of the most popular drinks in 1800s America; it helped make the drinking straw popular.
