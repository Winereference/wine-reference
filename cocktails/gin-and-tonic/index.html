Gin and Tonic

Gin & juniper · Built

Crisp, bitter and refreshing; the gin's botanicals do the talking.

BaseGin
MethodBuilt
GlassHighball or large wine glass
GarnishLime or lemon wedge, or a botanical that suits the gin

Ingredients

  • 50 ml gin
  • 150 ml cold tonic water

How to make it

Fill the glass with ice, add the gin, then pour in the tonic gently down a bar spoon to keep the bubbles. Stir once.

History

British officers in India mixed gin with quinine tonic, taken against malaria, in the 1800s.
