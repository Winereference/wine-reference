Gimlet

Gin & juniper · Shaken

Lime-sharp and bracing, with juniper underneath.

BaseGin
MethodShaken
GlassCoupe
GarnishLime wheel

Ingredients

  • 60 ml gin
  • 20 ml fresh lime juice
  • 15 ml sugar syrup (or 20 ml lime cordial instead of both)

How to make it

Shake everything hard with ice and fine-strain into a chilled coupe.

History

Linked to the Royal Navy, whose sailors drank preserved lime juice against scurvy; lime cordial made it easy to mix.
