Boulevardier

Whisky · Stirred

A whiskey Negroni: warmer, rounder and less sharp.

BaseBourbon or rye
MethodStirred
GlassRocks glass or coupe
GarnishOrange peel

Ingredients

  • 45 ml bourbon or rye
  • 30 ml Campari
  • 30 ml sweet red vermouth

How to make it

Stir with ice and strain over a large ice cube or into a chilled coupe.

History

Linked to Erskine Gwynne, an American writer in Paris; it appeared in a 1927 book by Harry MacElhone of Harry's New York Bar.
