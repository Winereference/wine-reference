Bloody Mary

Vodka · Rolled

Savoury and spicy: tomato, lemon, Worcestershire and pepper.

BaseVodka
MethodRolled
GlassHighball
GarnishCelery stick and lemon wedge

Ingredients

  • 45 ml vodka
  • 90 ml tomato juice
  • 15 ml fresh lemon juice
  • 3 dashes Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 dashes Tabasco
  • Pinch of celery salt and black pepper

How to make it

Pour everything into a shaker with ice and 'roll' it gently back and forth between two tins (shaking makes tomato juice frothy). Strain into an ice-filled glass.

History

Fernand Petiot said he first made it at Harry's New York Bar in Paris in the 1920s.
