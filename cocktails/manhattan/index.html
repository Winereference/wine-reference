Manhattan

Whisky · Stirred

Rich and rounded: spicy rye, sweet vermouth and bitters.

BaseRye whiskey or bourbon
MethodStirred
GlassCoupe or martini glass
GarnishCocktail cherry

Ingredients

  • 50 ml rye whiskey
  • 20 ml sweet red vermouth
  • 1 dash Angostura bitters

How to make it

Stir with ice and strain into a chilled glass.

History

A New York drink of the 1870s or 1880s; the story that it was invented at the Manhattan Club is disputed.
