What wine goes with mozzarella in carrozza?

Cheese

Best wines for mozzarella in carrozza: Falanghina, Prosecco (Glera), Greco. Fried mozzarella sandwiches from Campania suit local crisp whites or fizz, whose acidity and bubbles cut the oil.

Why these grapes work

  • Falanghina (white): pear, citrus, floral. Best known in Campania.
  • Prosecco (Glera) (sparkling): pear, honeysuckle, melon. Best known in Veneto, Italy.
  • Greco (white): peach, pear, citrus. Best known in Campania.

Bottles to try with mozzarella in carrozza

  • Feudi di San Gregorio Falanghina del Sannio (Campania, Italy)
  • Bisol Crede Valdobbiadene Prosecco Superiore (Veneto, Italy)
  • Mastroberardino Greco di Tufo Novaserra (Campania, Italy)
