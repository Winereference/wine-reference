What wine goes with prawn cocktail?

Fish & seafood

Best wines for prawn cocktail: Sauvignon Blanc, Champagne (Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, Meunier). Zesty Sauvignon Blanc or brut Champagne refresh the palate after creamy Marie Rose sauce and complement sweet prawns.

Why these grapes work

  • Sauvignon Blanc (white): grapefruit, lime, fresh herbs. Best known in Loire, Marlborough, Bordeaux.
  • Champagne (Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, Meunier) (sparkling): brioche, green apple, citrus. Best known in Champagne, England.

Bottles to try with prawn cocktail

  • Cloudy Bay Sauvignon Blanc (Marlborough, New Zealand)
  • Brancott Estate Sauvignon Blanc (Marlborough, New Zealand)
  • Taittinger Brut Réserve (Champagne, France)
