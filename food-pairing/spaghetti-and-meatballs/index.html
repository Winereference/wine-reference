What wine goes with spaghetti and meatballs?

Pasta, rice & grains

Best wines for spaghetti and meatballs: Sangiovese, Montepulciano, Primitivo. Tomato sauce needs a red with good acidity, so Sangiovese or Montepulciano match the sauce while their fruit suits the meatballs.

Why these grapes work

  • Sangiovese (red): sour cherry, tomato leaf, herbs. Best known in Chianti, Montalcino.
  • Montepulciano (red): plum, black cherry, spice. Best known in Abruzzo, Marche.
  • Primitivo (red): blackberry, jam, spice. Best known in Puglia.

Bottles to try with spaghetti and meatballs

  • Fontodi Chianti Classico (Tuscany, Italy)
  • Farnese Montepulciano d'Abruzzo (Abruzzo, Italy)
  • Gianfranco Fino Es Primitivo di Manduria (Puglia, Italy)
