What wine goes with butternut squash soup?

Vegetarian & vegan

Best wines for butternut squash soup: Viognier, Chardonnay. Velvety squash matches full-bodied whites; Viognier's apricot and Chardonnay's creamy texture echo the soup's sweetness.

Why these grapes work

  • Viognier (white): peach, apricot, honeysuckle. Best known in Rhône.
  • Chardonnay (white): apple, lemon, butter, oak. Best known in Burgundy, California, Australia.

Bottles to try with butternut squash soup

  • Yalumba Y Series Viognier (South Australia, Australia)
  • Georges Vernay Condrieu Les Terrasses de l'Empire (Rhône, France)
  • Kendall-Jackson Vintner's Reserve Chardonnay (California, USA)
