What wine goes with chicken tagine with preserved lemon and olives?

Poultry & game

Best wines for chicken tagine with preserved lemon and olives: Viognier, Grenache Rosé. Aromatic Viognier and dry Provence rosé handle the saffron, ginger and salty olives without clashing with the preserved lemon.

Why these grapes work

  • Viognier (white): peach, apricot, honeysuckle. Best known in Rhône.
  • Grenache Rosé (rosé): strawberry, melon, herbs. Best known in Provence, Spain.

Bottles to try with chicken tagine with preserved lemon and olives

  • Yalumba Eden Valley Viognier (Eden Valley, Australia)
  • Château Minuty M de Minuty Rosé (Provence, France)
  • Domaine Tempier Bandol Rosé (Bandol, France)
