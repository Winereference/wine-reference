What wine goes with crispy duck pancakes?

Spicy & world

Best wines for crispy duck pancakes: Pinot Noir, Gewürztraminer. Pinot Noir's cherry fruit matches sweet hoisin and rich duck, while spicy Gewürztraminer picks up five-spice and plum.

Why these grapes work

  • Pinot Noir (red): red cherry, earth, mushroom. Best known in Burgundy, Oregon, New Zealand.
  • Gewürztraminer (white): lychee, rose, spice. Best known in Alsace.

Bottles to try with crispy duck pancakes

  • Ata Rangi Crimson Pinot Noir (Martinborough, New Zealand)
  • Brancott Estate Pinot Noir (Marlborough, New Zealand)
  • Zind-Humbrecht Gewurztraminer (Alsace, France)
