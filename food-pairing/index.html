Food and wine pairing

50 dishes with the grapes that suit them, real bottles to try and a recipe.

Meat

Steak

Wine: Cabernet Sauvignon, Syrah / Shiraz, Tempranillo

Firm tannins in Cabernet and Tempranillo cut through the fat and char of a seared steak, while peppery Shiraz echoes the sauce.

Try: Penfolds Bin 389 Cabernet Shiraz; Marqués de Riscal Rioja Reserva; Concha y Toro Casillero del Diablo Cabernet Sauvignon

Recipe: Steak with peppercorn sauce

Beef bourguignon

Wine: Pinot Noir, Gamay

The beef is braised in red Burgundy, so Pinot Noir mirrors its earthy mushroom and bacon flavours without overpowering the tender meat.

Try: Bouchard Père & Fils Beaune du Château Premier Cru; Louis Jadot Beaujolais-Villages Combe aux Jacques

Recipe: Beef Bourguignon

Roast leg of lamb

Wine: Cabernet Sauvignon, Tempranillo, Merlot

Lamb's rich fat and rosemary call for structured reds; Cabernet's cassis and cedar and Rioja's savoury oak are the classic partners.

Try: Château Lynch-Bages Pauillac; CVNE Viña Real Rioja Crianza; Cono Sur Bicicleta Cabernet Sauvignon

Recipe: Roast Leg of Lamb with Rosemary

Shepherd's pie

Wine: Syrah / Shiraz, Garnacha / Grenache

Peppery, juicy Grenache and Shiraz match savoury lamb mince and gravy without overwhelming a homely dish.

Try: Guigal Côtes du Rhône Rouge; Wolf Blass Yellow Label Shiraz; Bodegas Borsao Borsao Selección Tinto

Recipe: Shepherd's pie

Chilli con carne

Wine: Malbec, Zinfandel

Ripe, plush, low-tannin reds like Malbec and Zinfandel soften chilli heat and match the dish's smoky spice and beans.

Try: Catena Malbec; Trapiche Oak Cask Malbec; Seghesio Sonoma Zinfandel

Recipe: Chilli con carne

Toad in the hole

Wine: Merlot, Garnacha / Grenache

Soft, juicy Merlot and Grenache have enough fruit for pork sausages and onion gravy, with gentle tannins that suit the batter.

Try: Concha y Toro Casillero del Diablo Merlot; Famille Perrin Réserve Côtes du Rhône Rouge; Las Rocas Garnacha

Recipe: Easy toad in the hole

Osso buco

Wine: Nebbiolo, Barbera

Northern Italian reds suit this Milanese braise: Barbera's acidity cuts the rich marrow, and Nebbiolo's tannin frames slow-cooked veal.

Try: Produttori del Barbaresco Barbaresco; G.D. Vajra Langhe Nebbiolo; Michele Chiarlo Le Orme Barbera d'Asti Superiore

Recipe: Osso Buco

Poultry & game

Roast chicken

Wine: Chardonnay, Merlot, Pinot Noir

Roast chicken is a versatile partner; lightly oaked Chardonnay or a soft, low-tannin Pinot Noir or Merlot suits crisp skin and savoury gravy.

Try: Louis Latour Mâcon-Lugny Les Genièvres; Château Ste. Michelle Columbia Valley Merlot; Villa Maria Private Bin Pinot Noir

Recipe: Classic roast chicken & gravy

Coq au vin

Wine: Pinot Noir, Gamay

Red Burgundy or Beaujolais suits chicken braised in wine with mushrooms and lardons, adding red-berry fruit and earthy notes.

Try: Faiveley Mercurey La Framboisière; Château Thivin Côte de Brouilly; Louis Latour Bourgogne Pinot Noir

Recipe: Coq Au Vin

Duck breast with cherry sauce

Wine: Pinot Noir, Merlot

Pinot Noir's cherry fruit echoes the sauce and its acidity cuts rich duck fat, while plush Merlot suits the sweeter side of the dish.

Try: Felton Road Bannockburn Pinot Noir; Domaine Drouhin Oregon Dundee Hills Pinot Noir; Duckhorn Napa Valley Merlot

Recipe: Seared Duck Breasts with Sour Cherry Sauce

Roast turkey

Wine: Pinot Noir, Gamay, Chardonnay

Lean turkey and its many trimmings need light, fruity, fresh wines; Pinot Noir, Beaujolais or Chablis won't overpower the meat.

Try: Marcel Lapierre Morgon; Cono Sur Bicicleta Pinot Noir; William Fèvre Chablis

Recipe: Roast turkey with apricot stuffing

Venison stew

Wine: Syrah / Shiraz, Garnacha / Grenache

Gamey venison loves Syrah's dark fruit, pepper and smoky notes, and Grenache-led Rhône blends add warmth for a winter stew.

Try: Château de Beaucastel Châteauneuf-du-Pape; Paul Jaboulet Aîné Crozes-Hermitage Les Jalets; d'Arenberg The Footbolt Shiraz

Recipe: Venison Stew

Chicken Kiev

Wine: Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc

Garlic butter and crisp crumbs want a fresh white; Chablis-style Chardonnay or zesty Sauvignon Blanc cut the richness and match the herbs.

Try: Kumeu River Village Chardonnay; Domaine Laroche Chablis Saint Martin; Villa Maria Private Bin Sauvignon Blanc

Recipe: Chicken Kiev

Fish & seafood

Salmon

Wine: Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, Grenache Rosé

Salmon is rich enough for a light red: low-tannin Pinot Noir won't clash with the oily fish, and creamy Chardonnay matches its texture.

Try: Louis Jadot Bourgogne Pinot Noir; Cloudy Bay Chardonnay; Château Miraval Côtes de Provence Rosé

Recipe: Pan-fried salmon

Oysters

Wine: Champagne (Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, Meunier), Sauvignon Blanc, Melon de Bourgogne

Briny oysters love crisp, mineral whites: Champagne's bubbles, Muscadet's saline edge and Sancerre's citrus all act like a squeeze of lemon.

Try: Domaine de la Pépière Muscadet Sèvre et Maine; Pol Roger Brut Réserve; Henri Bourgeois Sancerre Les Baronnes

Recipe: Raw Oysters with Mignonette Sauce

Fish and chips

Wine: Champagne (Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, Meunier), Sauvignon Blanc

Crisp acidity and bubbles slice through batter like a squeeze of lemon, which is why fizz with fish and chips is a British favourite.

Try: Moët & Chandon Brut Impérial; Nyetimber Classic Cuvee; Oyster Bay Sauvignon Blanc

Recipe: Beer Battered Fish and Chips

Moules marinières

Wine: Melon de Bourgogne, Sauvignon Blanc

Mussels cooked in white wine, shallots and parsley suit light, saline Loire whites, especially Muscadet from near the Atlantic coast.

Try: Domaine Luneau-Papin Muscadet Sèvre et Maine Clos des Allées; Pascal Jolivet Sancerre

Recipe: Moules marinière

Grilled sea bass

Wine: Albariño, Vermentino, Assyrtiko

Delicate grilled fish wants crisp coastal whites; Albariño, Vermentino and Assyrtiko bring citrus and a sea-spray freshness.

Try: Pazo de Señorans Albariño; Argiolas Costamolino Vermentino di Sardegna; Domaine Sigalas Assyrtiko

Recipe: Whole Grilled Sea Bass

Prawn cocktail

Wine: Sauvignon Blanc, Champagne (Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, Meunier)

Zesty Sauvignon Blanc or brut Champagne refresh the palate after creamy Marie Rose sauce and complement sweet prawns.

Try: Cloudy Bay Sauvignon Blanc; Brancott Estate Sauvignon Blanc; Taittinger Brut Réserve

Recipe: Classic prawn cocktail

Fish pie

Wine: Chardonnay, Chenin Blanc

Creamy sauce and buttery mash need a rounded white with fresh acidity, such as unoaked Burgundy Chardonnay or Chenin Blanc.

Try: Louis Jadot Mâcon-Villages; Ken Forrester Old Vine Reserve Chenin Blanc; Domaine Huet Vouvray Le Haut-Lieu Sec

Recipe: Fish pie

Vegetarian & vegan

Ratatouille

Wine: Grenache Rosé, Garnacha / Grenache

A Provençal dish deserves Provençal wines: dry rosé or a supple Grenache red matches the herbs, tomato and olive oil.

Try: Domaine Tempier Bandol Rosé; Château Minuty M de Minuty Rosé; M. Chapoutier Belleruche Côtes du Rhône Rouge

Recipe: Ratatouille

Aubergine parmigiana

Wine: Nero d'Avola, Montepulciano

Southern Italian reds with ripe fruit and fresh acidity stand up to tomato, aubergine and melted cheese without heavy tannin.

Try: Donnafugata Sherazade Nero d'Avola; Planeta La Segreta Rosso; Masciarelli Montepulciano d'Abruzzo

Recipe: Claudio's aubergine parmigiana

Falafel

Wine: Sauvignon Blanc, Grenache Rosé

Herby, crunchy falafel with tahini suits zesty Sauvignon Blanc or a crisp dry rosé, both fresh enough for lemon and garlic.

Try: Babich Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc; Gérard Bertrand Gris Blanc

Recipe: Falafel

Butternut squash soup

Wine: Viognier, Chardonnay

Velvety squash matches full-bodied whites; Viognier's apricot and Chardonnay's creamy texture echo the soup's sweetness.

Try: Yalumba Y Series Viognier; Georges Vernay Condrieu Les Terrasses de l'Empire; Kendall-Jackson Vintner's Reserve Chardonnay

Recipe: Roasted Butternut Squash Soup

Cauliflower cheese

Wine: Chardonnay, Pinot Blanc

Cheesy béchamel loves a creamy yet fresh white; Burgundy Chardonnay or Alsace Pinot Blanc match the richness without clashing.

Try: Bouchard Père & Fils Bourgogne Chardonnay; Hugel Pinot Blanc Cuvée Les Amours

Recipe: Cauliflower Cheese

Pasta, rice & grains

Pasta & tomato

Wine: Sangiovese, Pinot Grigio

Tomato's high acidity needs an equally fresh wine; Sangiovese's bright cherry fruit and acidity is a natural match for tomato sauce.

Try: Ruffino Chianti; Fontodi Chianti Classico; Alois Lageder Pinot Grigio

Recipe: Fresh tomato pasta

Pizza

Wine: Sangiovese, Prosecco (Glera)

Sangiovese's acidity matches tomato and cuts melted mozzarella, and Prosecco's bubbles keep a salty, cheesy slice tasting fresh.

Try: Banfi Rosso di Montalcino; Antinori Pèppoli Chianti Classico; Bisol Crede Prosecco Superiore

Recipe: Ultimate pizza Margherita

Spaghetti carbonara

Wine: Trebbiano, Verdicchio

Crisp Italian whites with almond and citrus notes cut through egg, Pecorino and guanciale; Frascati is Rome's own pairing.

Try: Fontana Candida Frascati Superiore; Umani Ronchi Casal di Serra Verdicchio dei Castelli di Jesi Classico Superiore; Bucci Verdicchio dei Castelli di Jesi Classico Superiore

Recipe: Spaghetti Carbonara (original recipe)

Lasagne

Wine: Sangiovese, Montepulciano, Barbera

Rich meat ragù and béchamel need medium-bodied Italian reds whose high acidity balances tomato and cuts through cheese.

Try: Antinori Villa Antinori Rosso; Umani Ronchi Jorio Montepulciano d'Abruzzo; Fontanafredda Briccotondo Barbera

Recipe: Classic lasagne

Seafood paella

Wine: Albariño, Verdejo, Grenache Rosé

Spanish whites like Albariño and Verdejo bring citrus and salinity for shellfish and saffron, while dry rosado suits paella's crust.

Try: Martín Códax Albariño; Marqués de Riscal Rueda Verdejo; Bodegas Muga Rosado

Recipe: Easy Seafood Paella

Mushroom risotto

Wine: Pinot Noir, Nebbiolo, Chardonnay

Earthy mushrooms and Parmesan pick up the forest-floor notes of Pinot Noir and Nebbiolo, or the creamy texture of Chardonnay.

Try: Joseph Drouhin Laforêt Bourgogne Pinot Noir; Pio Cesare Langhe Nebbiolo; Elena Walch Chardonnay

Recipe: Mushroom Risotto

Spaghetti alle vongole

Wine: Falanghina, Fiano, Vermentino

Clams, garlic and chilli suit crisp, saline southern Italian whites that mirror the briny juices and lift the olive oil.

Try: Feudi di San Gregorio Falanghina del Sannio; Mastroberardino Fiano di Avellino; Sella & Mosca La Cala Vermentino di Sardegna

Recipe: Spaghetti alle vongole

Spicy & world

Spicy curry

Wine: Riesling, Grenache Rosé

A touch of sweetness and low alcohol in off-dry Riesling cool chilli heat, while fruity rosé handles aromatic spice without turning bitter.

Try: Dr. Loosen Dr. L Riesling; Pewsey Vale Eden Valley Riesling; Château d'Esclans Whispering Angel

Recipe: Easy chicken curry

Pad Thai

Wine: Riesling, Gewürztraminer

Sweet, sour, salty and spicy flavours suit aromatic whites; Riesling's lime acidity and Gewürztraminer's lychee match tamarind and peanuts.

Try: Jim Barry The Lodge Hill Riesling; Pewsey Vale Eden Valley Riesling; Hugel Gewurztraminer Classic

Recipe: Pad Thai noodles

Thai green curry

Wine: Gewürztraminer, Riesling

Coconut, lemongrass and green chilli suit off-dry, aromatic whites whose touch of sweetness tames heat and echoes the herbs.

Try: Trimbach Gewurztraminer; Selbach-Oster Riesling Kabinett

Recipe: Green chicken curry

Lamb tagine with apricots

Wine: Garnacha / Grenache, Syrah / Shiraz, Carignan

Warm spices, apricots and lamb suit ripe, spicy Grenache and Syrah blends, whose soft fruit embraces the sweetness.

Try: Álvaro Palacios Camins del Priorat; Château de Saint Cosme Côtes du Rhône; Torbreck Woodcutter's Shiraz

Recipe: Lamb and apricot tagine

Beef tacos

Wine: Malbec, Tempranillo

Juicy, fruit-forward reds with soft tannins match spiced beef, salsa and cheese, and won't turn bitter against a little chilli.

Try: Zuccardi Serie A Malbec; Bodega Norton Reserva Malbec; Campo Viejo Rioja Tempranillo

Recipe: Classic Ground Beef Tacos

Kung pao chicken

Wine: Riesling, Grenache Rosé

Sichuan pepper, chilli and peanuts need fruity, refreshing wines; off-dry Riesling and fuller rosé handle the heat and sweet-sour glaze.

Try: Charles Smith Kung Fu Girl Riesling; Château d'Aquéria Tavel Rosé

Recipe: Kung pao chicken

Sushi rolls (maki)

Wine: Champagne (Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, Meunier), Grüner Veltliner

Delicate raw fish, rice vinegar and soy suit crisp, unoaked wines; Champagne and peppery Grüner Veltliner refresh without masking the fish.

Try: Veuve Clicquot Yellow Label Brut; Domäne Wachau Grüner Veltliner Federspiel Terrassen; Laurenz V. Singing Grüner Veltliner

Recipe: How to Make Sushi at Home

Crispy duck pancakes

Wine: Pinot Noir, Gewürztraminer

Pinot Noir's cherry fruit matches sweet hoisin and rich duck, while spicy Gewürztraminer picks up five-spice and plum.

Try: Ata Rangi Crimson Pinot Noir; Brancott Estate Pinot Noir; Zind-Humbrecht Gewurztraminer

Recipe: Duck pancakes

Cheese

Cheese board

Wine: Prosecco (Glera), Merlot, Riesling

Mixed cheeses need a mixed line-up: Riesling for soft and blue cheeses, soft Merlot for hard cheeses, and Prosecco's bubbles to refresh.

Try: Mionetto Prosecco Treviso Brut; André Lurton Château Bonnet Rouge; Trimbach Riesling

Recipe: How to Make a Cheese Board

Cheese fondue

Wine: Chasselas, Petite Arvine

In Switzerland fondue comes with the local white: light, dry Chasselas cleanses the palate between mouthfuls of melted Gruyère.

Try: Louis Bovard Dézaley Médinette; Henri Badoux Aigle Les Murailles

Recipe: Swiss Cheese Fondue

Baked camembert

Wine: Chardonnay, Gamay

Gooey camembert needs acidity: crisp unoaked Chablis cuts the richness, while light, fruity Beaujolais suits garlic and rosemary.

Try: Jean-Marc Brocard Chablis Sainte Claire; Georges Duboeuf Fleurie

Recipe: Whole camembert baked with garlic and rosemary

Warm goat's cheese salad

Wine: Sauvignon Blanc, Chenin Blanc

Goat's cheese and Loire Sauvignon Blanc are a classic match: the wine's tangy acidity and herbal notes mirror the cheese's freshness.

Try: Domaine Vacheron Sancerre; Alphonse Mellot Sancerre La Moussière; Château de Tracy Pouilly-Fumé

Recipe: Warm French Goat's Cheese Salad (Salade de Chèvre Chaud)

Desserts

Tiramisu

Wine: Muscat, Pedro Ximénez

Light, sweet, low-alcohol Moscato d'Asti refreshes after mascarpone, while raisiny Pedro Ximénez sherry echoes the coffee and cocoa.

Try: Vietti Moscato d'Asti Cascinetta; Saracco Moscato d'Asti; González Byass Noé Pedro Ximénez 30 Years Old

Recipe: Classic tiramisu

Chocolate fondant

Wine: Touriga Nacional, Garnacha / Grenache

Chocolate overwhelms most dry wines, so choose a sweet fortified red; ruby or LBV Port has the sweetness and dark fruit to match.

Try: Graham's Six Grapes Reserve Port; Taylor's Late Bottled Vintage Port

Recipe: Chocolate fondant

Crème brûlée

Wine: Sémillon, Muscat

Sauternes and botrytised Sémillon bring honey, apricot and acidity that match vanilla custard and caramelised sugar.

Try: Château Suduiraut Sauternes; De Bortoli Noble One Botrytis Semillon; Domaine de Durban Muscat de Beaumes-de-Venise

Recipe: Easy Crème Brûlée

Sticky toffee pudding

Wine: Pedro Ximénez, Furmint, Muscat

Dates and toffee sauce need an even sweeter wine; PX sherry, Rutherglen Muscat or Tokaji Aszú match the caramel and dried-fruit notes.

Try: Lustau Pedro Ximénez San Emilio; Royal Tokaji Tokaji Aszú 5 Puttonyos; Campbells Rutherglen Muscat

Recipe: Sticky Toffee Pudding

Snacks & party

Smoked salmon blinis

Wine: Champagne (Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, Meunier), Chardonnay

Champagne is the classic canapé partner: bubbles and acidity cut oily smoked salmon and crème fraîche, and toasty notes suit the blini.

Try: Ruinart Blanc de Blancs; Laurent-Perrier La Cuvée Brut; Ridgeview Bloomsbury

Recipe: Smoked salmon blinis with caviar

Gougères

Wine: Aligoté, Chardonnay

Burgundy's cheesy choux puffs are traditionally served with local whites; crisp Aligoté or Chablis cut the Gruyère richness.

Try: A. et P. de Villaine Bouzeron; Louis Jadot Chablis; Simonnet-Febvre Crémant de Bourgogne Brut

Recipe: Gougères (French cheese puffs)
