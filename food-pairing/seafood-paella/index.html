What wine goes with seafood paella?

Pasta, rice & grains

Best wines for seafood paella: Albariño, Verdejo, Grenache Rosé. Spanish whites like Albariño and Verdejo bring citrus and salinity for shellfish and saffron, while dry rosado suits paella's crust.

Why these grapes work

  • Albariño (white): peach, citrus, saline. Best known in Rías Baixas, Vinho Verde.
  • Verdejo (white): citrus, herbs, fennel. Best known in Rueda.
  • Grenache Rosé (rosé): strawberry, melon, herbs. Best known in Provence, Spain.

Bottles to try with seafood paella

  • Martín Códax Albariño (Rías Baixas, Spain)
  • Marqués de Riscal Rueda Verdejo (Rueda, Spain)
  • Bodegas Muga Rosado (Rioja, Spain)
