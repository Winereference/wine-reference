What wine goes with grilled swordfish?

Fish & seafood

Best wines for grilled swordfish: Vermentino, Assyrtiko, Nero d'Avola. Meaty swordfish takes a fuller, saline Mediterranean white or even a light, chilled Sicilian red.

Why these grapes work

  • Vermentino (white): citrus, pear, herbs. Best known in Sardinia, Liguria.
  • Assyrtiko (white): lemon, mineral, saline. Best known in Santorini.
  • Nero d'Avola (red): plum, blackberry, pepper. Best known in Sicily.

Bottles to try with grilled swordfish

  • Argiolas Costamolino Vermentino di Sardegna (Sardinia, Italy)
  • Estate Argyros Santorini Assyrtiko (Santorini, Greece)
  • Donnafugata Sherazade Nero d'Avola (Sicily, Italy)
