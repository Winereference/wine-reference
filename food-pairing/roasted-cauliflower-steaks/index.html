What wine goes with roasted cauliflower steaks?

Vegetarian & vegan

Best wines for roasted cauliflower steaks: Chenin Blanc, Chardonnay. Textured whites with nutty, appley notes suit the caramelised edges of roasted cauliflower without overwhelming its gentle flavour.

Why these grapes work

  • Chenin Blanc (white): apple, quince, honey. Best known in Loire, South Africa.
  • Chardonnay (white): apple, lemon, butter, oak. Best known in Burgundy, California, Australia.

Bottles to try with roasted cauliflower steaks

  • Ken Forrester Old Vine Reserve Chenin Blanc (Stellenbosch, South Africa)
  • Domaine Huet Vouvray Le Haut-Lieu Sec (Loire, France)
  • Leeuwin Estate Prelude Vineyards Chardonnay (Margaret River, Australia)
