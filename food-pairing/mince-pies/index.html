What wine goes with mince pies?

Desserts

Best wines for mince pies: Touriga Nacional, Muscat, Malvasia. Tawny Port, Muscat or Malmsey are sweeter than the mincemeat and echo its dried fruit, peel and spice, a classic Christmas sideboard match.

Why these grapes work

  • Touriga Nacional (red): blackberry, violet, spice. Best known in Douro, Dão.
  • Muscat (white): floral, peach, grape. Best known in Alsace, Italy.
  • Malvasia (white): peach, floral, almond. Best known in Italy, Iberia.

Bottles to try with mince pies

  • Graham's 20 Year Old Tawny (Douro, Portugal)
  • Domaine des Bernardins Muscat de Beaumes de Venise (Rhône, France)
  • Blandy's 10 Year Old Malmsey Madeira (Madeira, Portugal)
