What wine goes with lamb biryani?

Spicy & world

Best wines for lamb biryani: Grenache, Syrah / Shiraz, Gewürztraminer. Fragrant spices and tender lamb suit soft, fruity Grenache-based reds or a perfumed Gewurztraminer that echoes cardamom and saffron.

Why these grapes work

  • Garnacha / Grenache (red): strawberry, cherry, spice. Best known in Spain, Rhône, Sardinia.
  • Syrah / Shiraz (red): blackberry, pepper, smoke. Best known in Rhône, Barossa, Washington.
  • Gewürztraminer (white): lychee, rose, spice. Best known in Alsace.

Bottles to try with lamb biryani

  • Famille Perrin Côtes du Rhône Réserve Rouge (Rhône Valley, France)
  • Yalumba Old Bush Vine Grenache (Barossa Valley, Australia)
  • Trimbach Gewurztraminer (Alsace, France)
