Privacy policy

This page explains what information The Wine Reference collects, why, and the choices you have. Last updated: 16 November 2026.

Who runs this site

The Wine Reference (thewinereference.co.uk) is run by Diego Muntoni in London, United Kingdom, who is responsible for your data under UK data protection law. Contact: finewineloversuk@gmail.com.

Your choices

Visitors in the UK, EU and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message before cookies are used for analytics or advertising. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the button below, or with 'Privacy and cookie settings' at the bottom of every page.

Analytics (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics to see how visitors use the site: pages viewed, device and browser type, and approximate location. In the UK, EU and Switzerland it uses cookies (such as _ga) only if you consent; without consent it sends only basic, cookieless measurements. We use it only to improve the guide. Google acts as our processor; see Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

The site shows ads from Google AdSense, which helps keep it free. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and, with your consent, personalise them. Visitors in the UK, EU and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent message before personal data is used for ads. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services, and manage personalised ads at Google Ad Settings.

Newsletter (Kit)

If you sign up for the newsletter, your email address is held by our email provider, Kit, to send it to you. You confirm the sign-up by email, and every email has an unsubscribe link. The legal basis is your consent.

Support payments (Ko-fi)

The 'Buy me a glass' buttons go to Ko-fi. Payments are handled by Ko-fi and its payment partners (PayPal or Stripe); we never see your card details. We may see your name and any message you choose to leave.

Member accounts

If member accounts are switched on and you create one, we store your email address and the details you add (such as a business name) so we can give you access to members' areas. Passwords are stored securely by our account provider and are never visible to us. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.

Stored only on your device

Some features remember your choices in your own browser: favourite grapes and regions, flashcard progress, your GP calculator wine list, text size and your cookie choice. This information stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can clear it in your browser settings.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see GitHub's Privacy Statement. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers when a page loads.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict how it is used, and to withdraw consent at any time. To use these rights, email us at the address above. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Age

The Wine Reference is about alcohol and is intended for adults of legal drinking age (18 in the UK).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
