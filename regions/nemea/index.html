Nemea wine region

Greece · Europe

Greece's largest red-wine appellation, all Agiorgitiko, grown from valley floor to high plateau in the north-east Peloponnese.

Ancient winemaking and many native grapes, from island whites to northern reds.

CountryGreece
Key grapesAgiorgitiko
SubregionsKoutsi, Asprokambos, Gymno, Ancient Nemea, Leontio, Psari, Petri, Ancient Kleones
