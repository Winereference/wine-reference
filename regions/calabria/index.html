Cirò Classico (2025)
Red from the historic heart of Cirò, the communes of Cirò and Cirò Marina, on sunny clay and limestone hills along the Ionian coast of Calabria. At least 90% Gaglioppo with up to 10% Magliocco or Greco Nero, aged 36 months including 6 in wood, with at least 13% alcohol. Good to know: approved in July 2025, it is Calabria's first DOCG; rosato and white Cirò stay under the Cirò DOC.		DOCGRedGaglioppo, Magliocco
