Beaujolais wine region

Photo: Carnet de Voyage d'Alex on Unsplash

France · Europe

Gamay country: juicy Beaujolais Nouveau at one end, ten granite-grown crus at the other.

The benchmark for many wine styles, organized by appellation (AOC).

CountryFrance
Key grapesGamay, Chardonnay
SubregionsBrouilly, Côte de Brouilly, Régnié, Morgon, Chiroubles, Fleurie, Moulin-à-Vent, Chénas, Juliénas, Saint-Amour

Fleurie (1936)
Perfumed, silky cru from pink granite slopes around the village chapel of La Madone.		AOCRedGamay
