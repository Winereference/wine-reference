Climate and soil

Tropical and semi-arid, near the equator, with irrigation.

Key areas and appellations

Vale do São Francisco

Good to know

Vines can give more than one harvest a year, an unusual tropical wine region.

The grapes of Vale do São Francisco

  • Syrah / Shiraz (red): blackberry, pepper, smoke. Best known in Rhône, Barossa, Washington.
  • Chenin Blanc (white): apple, quince, honey. Best known in Loire, South Africa.

Notable producers in Vale do São Francisco

  • Miolo Terranova
  • Vinibrasil
  • Rio Sol

Food to try with Vale do São Francisco wines

  • Steak: Firm tannins in Cabernet and Tempranillo cut through the fat and char of a seared steak, while peppery Shiraz echoes the sauce.
  • Shepherd's pie: Peppery, juicy Grenache and Shiraz match savoury lamb mince and gravy without overwhelming a homely dish.
  • Venison stew: Gamey venison loves Syrah's dark fruit, pepper and smoky notes, and Grenache-led Rhône blends add warmth for a winter stew.
  • Fish pie: Creamy sauce and buttery mash need a rounded white with fresh acidity, such as unoaked Burgundy Chardonnay or Chenin Blanc.
  • Lamb tagine with apricots: Warm spices, apricots and lamb suit ripe, spicy Grenache and Syrah blends, whose soft fruit embraces the sweetness.
  • Warm goat's cheese salad: Goat's cheese and Loire Sauvignon Blanc are a classic match: the wine's tangy acidity and herbal notes mirror the cheese's freshness.
