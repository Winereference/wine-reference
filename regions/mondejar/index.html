Mondéjar wine region

Spain · Europe

Small appellation in Guadalajara, near Madrid.

The world's largest vineyard area, led by Tempranillo and Garnacha.

CountrySpain
Key grapesMalvar, Tempranillo

Climate and soil

Continental climate on the edge of the Tajo basin.

Key areas and appellations

Mondéjar DO

Good to know

One of Spain's smallest DOs, with whites from Malvar and reds from Tempranillo.

The grapes of Mondéjar

  • Tempranillo (red): cherry, leather, vanilla. Best known in Rioja, Ribera del Duero.

Food to try with Mondéjar wines

  • Steak: Firm tannins in Cabernet and Tempranillo cut through the fat and char of a seared steak, while peppery Shiraz echoes the sauce.
  • Roast leg of lamb: Lamb's rich fat and rosemary call for structured reds; Cabernet's cassis and cedar and Rioja's savoury oak are the classic partners.
  • Beef tacos: Juicy, fruit-forward reds with soft tannins match spiced beef, salsa and cheese, and won't turn bitter against a little chilli.
  • Shakshuka: Spiced tomato and peppers suit a dry rosé or a juicy, low-tannin red that won't clash with the cumin and chilli.
  • Patatas bravas: A tapas-bar classic with tapas-bar wines: chilled fino or a fruity Garnacha refreshes against crisp potatoes and smoky paprika sauce.
  • Irish stew: Gentle lamb and potato stew pairs with medium-bodied claret or Rioja, whose soft tannins and savoury notes suit slow-cooked lamb.
