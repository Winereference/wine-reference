Hawke's Bay wine region

Photo: James Coleman on Unsplash

New Zealand · Oceania

Warm, gravelly home of New Zealand's finest Bordeaux blends and Syrah.

Cool, maritime islands best known for vivid Sauvignon Blanc, Pinot Noir and Hawke's Bay reds.

CountryNew Zealand
Key grapesMerlot, Syrah, Cabernet Sauvignon, Chardonnay
SubregionsGimblett Gravels, Bridge Pa Triangle, Te Mata, Te Awanga, Central Hawke's Bay
