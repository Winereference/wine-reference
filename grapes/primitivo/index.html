Primitivo grape

Red grape

Primitivo is a red grape known for blackberry, jam, spice. Its best-known homes are Puglia.

StyleRed
Body●●●●● (5/5)
FlavoursBlackberry, jam, spice
Key regionsPuglia

Origin and history

Genetically identical to Zinfandel and Croatia's Tribidrag (Crljenak Kaštelanski); it reached Puglia by the late 1700s, where a priest in Gioia del Colle is credited with selecting it.

Where it grows

Puglia, chiefly Primitivo di Manduria and Gioia del Colle, also Salento IGT; the same vine is grown in California (as Zinfandel) and Croatia.

Structure

Full body, high alcohol (often 14–16%), soft to medium tannins and moderate acidity.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: blackberry jam, black cherry, plum, fig and sweet spice. With age: raisin, dried fig, liquorice and leather.

Ageing

Most best within 2–6 years; the finest Manduria and Gioia del Colle keep 8–12 years.

Also known as

Zinfandel, Tribidrag, Crljenak Kaštelanski.

Good to know

The name comes from 'primo' (first): it ripens early in the season, and Primitivo di Manduria Dolce Naturale is a sweet DOCG made from late-harvested grapes.
