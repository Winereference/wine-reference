Petite Arvine grape

White grape

Petite Arvine is a white grape known for grapefruit, saline, floral. Its best-known homes are Valais, Aosta Valley.

StyleWhite
Body●●●○○ (3/5)
FlavoursGrapefruit, saline, floral
Key regionsValais, Aosta Valley

Origin and history

A native of Switzerland's Valais, documented there since the early 17th century; its exact parentage remains unknown.

Where it grows

Valais in Switzerland (especially around Fully, Chamoson and Sion) and Italy's Aosta Valley (Valle d'Aosta DOC).

Structure

Medium to full body, high acidity and ample alcohol; dry, or sweet when made from late-harvested or dried grapes.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: grapefruit, rhubarb, white flowers, wisteria and a distinctive salty finish. With age: honey, quince, candied citrus and nutty notes.

Ageing

Dry wines 3–8 years; sweet styles can last 15 years or more.

Also known as

Arvine.

Good to know

Its trademark saline finish makes it a classic match for Alpine cheeses; the village of Fully is regarded as its spiritual home.
