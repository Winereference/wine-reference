Saperavi grape

Red grape

Saperavi is a red grape known for blackberry, plum, spice. Its best-known homes are Georgia.

StyleRed
Body●●●●● (5/5)
FlavoursBlackberry, plum, spice
Key regionsGeorgia

Origin and history

Native to Georgia, one of the world's oldest winemaking countries, where it has been grown for centuries in the eastern region of Kakheti.

Where it grows

Kakheti in Georgia (Mukuzani, Napareuli, Kindzmarauli, Akhasheni), plus Moldova, Ukraine, Russia, Armenia, Australia and New York's Finger Lakes.

Structure

Full body, inky colour, high acidity and firm tannins; also made semi-sweet in Kindzmarauli and Akhasheni.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: blackberry, black plum, liquorice and spice. With age: dried fruit, tobacco, chocolate, smoke and earth.

Ageing

Simpler wines drink in 3–5 years; top Mukuzani and qvevri-made wines can age 10–20 years or more.

Also known as

Saperavi Kakhuri.

Good to know

A rare teinturier with red flesh as well as skin; its name means 'dye' or 'paint' in Georgian, and it is often fermented in clay qvevri.
