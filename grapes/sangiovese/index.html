Sangiovese grape

Red grape

Sangiovese is a red grape known for sour cherry, tomato leaf, herbs. Its best-known homes are Chianti, Montalcino.

StyleRed
Body●●●○○ (3/5)
FlavoursSour cherry, tomato leaf, herbs
Key regionsChianti, Montalcino
Food pairingPizza, pasta, tomato sauces

Origin and history

Central Italy's great red grape, grown in Tuscany for centuries; its name is often linked to 'sanguis Jovis', the blood of Jupiter.

Where it grows

Chianti, Brunello di Montalcino, Vino Nobile di Montepulciano and Morellino di Scansano in Tuscany, plus Romagna, Umbria and Corsica.

Structure

Medium to full body, high acidity and firm tannins.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: sour cherry, red plum, tomato leaf and dried herbs. With age: leather, tobacco, earth and balsamic notes.

Ageing

Chianti 3–8 years; Brunello needs time and often ages 15–25 years.

Also known as

Brunello (Montalcino), Prugnolo Gentile (Montepulciano), Morellino (Scansano), Nielluccio (Corsica).

Good to know

Super Tuscans such as Tignanello blend it with Cabernet Sauvignon, which began a revolution in the 1970s.
