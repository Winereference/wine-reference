Cortese grape

White grape

Cortese is a white grape known for lemon, apple, mineral. Its best-known homes are Gavi, Piedmont.

StyleWhite
Body●●○○○ (2/5)
FlavoursLemon, apple, mineral
Key regionsGavi, Piedmont

Origin and history

A native of south-east Piedmont, documented there since the 17th century and long grown around the town of Gavi in the province of Alessandria.

Where it grows

Gavi DOCG in Piedmont, plus Colli Tortonesi, Monferrato, Oltrepò Pavese in Lombardy and Bianco di Custoza in the Veneto.

Structure

Light to medium body, crisp high acidity and modest alcohol; dry, lean and refreshing, usually unoaked.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: lemon, green apple, white flowers, almond and a flinty, mineral edge. With age: honey, hazelnut and lightly waxy notes.

Ageing

Best within 1–4 years; the finest Gavi can hold for 6–10 years.

Also known as

Bianca Fernanda (around Lake Garda).

Good to know

'Gavi di Gavi' (or Gavi del Comune di Gavi) on a label means the grapes came from the commune of Gavi itself; Gavi became a DOCG in 1998.
