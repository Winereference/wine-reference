Torrontés grape

White grape

Torrontés is a white grape known for peach, floral, rose. Its best-known homes are Argentina.

StyleWhite
Body●●●○○ (3/5)
FlavoursPeach, floral, rose
Key regionsArgentina

Origin and history

Argentina's signature white; DNA shows Torrontés Riojano is a natural cross of Muscat of Alexandria and Listán Prieto (Criolla Chica).

Where it grows

Argentina: Salta (Cafayate and the Calchaquí Valleys), La Rioja, Mendoza and San Juan. Spain's Torrontés is an unrelated grape.

Structure

Medium body, low to medium acidity and often high alcohol; usually dry despite its sweet-smelling, perfumed nose.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: rose petal, jasmine, peach, lychee and orange zest. Little benefit from age, losing its perfume and turning slightly bitter.

Ageing

Drink within 1–2 years for maximum aroma.

Also known as

Torrontés Riojano (also distinct Torrontés Sanjuanino and Torrontés Mendocino).

Good to know

Salta's vineyards are among the world's highest, some above 3,000 metres, and the intense sun gives Torrontés its vivid aromatics.
