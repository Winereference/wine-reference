País grape

Red grape

País is a red grape known for red cherry, strawberry, earth. Its best-known homes are Chile.

StyleRed
Body●●○○○ (2/5)
FlavoursRed cherry, strawberry, earth
Key regionsChile

Origin and history

Brought to the Americas by Spanish missionaries in the 16th century; DNA shows it is Listán Prieto, a now rare grape from Castilla-La Mancha.

Where it grows

Chile's southern regions, especially Maule, Itata and Bío-Bío; also California (as Mission), Argentina (as Criolla Chica) and the Canary Islands.

Structure

Light body, pale colour, rustic medium tannins and fresh acidity.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: red cherry, strawberry, raspberry, earth and herbs. With age: best fresh; dried berries and earthy notes.

Ageing

Most drink within 1–3 years; old-vine examples can hold 5 years.

Also known as

Listán Prieto (Spain), Mission (USA), Criolla Chica (Argentina).

Good to know

Chile's dry-farmed, ungrafted País vines are often well over 100 years old; it is the traditional grape for the rustic Pipeño style.
