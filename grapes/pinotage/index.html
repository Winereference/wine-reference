Pinotage grape

Red grape

Pinotage is a red grape known for blackberry, plum, smoke. Its best-known homes are South Africa.

StyleRed
Body●●●●○ (4/5)
FlavoursBlackberry, plum, smoke
Key regionsSouth Africa

Origin and history

Created in 1925 by Professor Abraham Perold at Stellenbosch University by crossing Pinot Noir with Cinsault, then known in South Africa as Hermitage.

Where it grows

South Africa (Stellenbosch, Swartland, Paarl, Walker Bay), with small plantings in New Zealand, Zimbabwe, California and Germany.

Structure

Medium to full body, deep colour, medium acidity and medium to high tannins.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: blackberry, black plum, mulberry, smoke and sometimes banana or coffee notes. With age: earth, leather, tobacco and dried fruit.

Ageing

Most best within 2–6 years; top examples, such as Kanonkop's, can age 10–20.

Good to know

Its name joins Pinot and Hermitage; the first commercial bottling was the 1959 Lanzerac, released in 1961, and 'coffee Pinotage' is a modern style.
