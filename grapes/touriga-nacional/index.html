Touriga Nacional grape

Red grape

Touriga Nacional is a red grape known for blackberry, violet, spice. Its best-known homes are Douro, Dão.

StyleRed
Body●●●●● (5/5)
FlavoursBlackberry, violet, spice
Key regionsDouro, Dão

Origin and history

Portugal's most celebrated red grape, probably native to the Dão; it nearly disappeared after phylloxera before a revival in the 1970s and 1980s.

Where it grows

Douro (for Port and dry reds), Dão, Alentejo and Lisbon, plus Australia, South Africa and California.

Structure

Full body, deep colour, high acidity and high, firm tannins.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: blackberry, black currant, violet, bergamot and rockrose, plus spice. With age: dried fruit, leather, tobacco and cedar.

Ageing

Dry reds best within 5–15 years; Vintage Port made with it can age 50 years or more.

Good to know

It is one of the five recommended grapes for Port, alongside Touriga Franca, Tinta Roriz, Tinta Barroca and Tinto Cão, and has tiny, low-yielding berries.
