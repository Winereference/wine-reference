Tempranillo grape

Red grape

Tempranillo is a red grape known for cherry, leather, vanilla. Its best-known homes are Rioja, Ribera del Duero.

StyleRed
Body●●●●○ (4/5)
FlavoursCherry, leather, vanilla
Key regionsRioja, Ribera del Duero
Food pairingChorizo, lamb, tapas

Origin and history

Spain's leading red grape; the name comes from 'temprano' (early), because it ripens early.

Where it grows

Rioja, Ribera del Duero, Toro, La Mancha and Navarra in Spain; the Douro and Alentejo in Portugal; and Argentina.

Structure

Medium to full body, medium tannin and medium acidity, with a smooth, savoury texture.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: strawberry, red cherry and plum. With oak and age: vanilla and coconut (American oak), leather, tobacco and dried fig.

Ageing

Rioja is labelled by ageing: Crianza (at least 2 years), Reserva (3) and Gran Reserva (5), and the best keep for decades.

Also known as

Tinto Fino or Tinta del País (Ribera del Duero), Tinta de Toro, Tinta Roriz or Aragonez (Portugal), Cencibel (La Mancha).

Good to know

One of the main grapes in Port, under its Portuguese name Tinta Roriz.
