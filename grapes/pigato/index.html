Pigato grape

White grape

Pigato is a white grape known for citrus, herbs, peach. Its best-known homes are Liguria.

StyleWhite
Body●●●○○ (3/5)
FlavoursCitrus, herbs, peach
Key regionsLiguria

Origin and history

The traditional white grape of western Liguria; DNA proves it is the same variety as Vermentino and Piedmont's Favorita.

Where it grows

Riviera Ligure di Ponente DOC, especially around Albenga and the Arroscia valley in the province of Savona and Imperia.

Structure

Medium body, medium acidity and a slightly oily, textured palate; dry with a gently bitter, saline finish.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: lemon, white peach, Mediterranean herbs, pine and sea air. With age: honey, almond and dried herbs.

Ageing

Best within 1–4 years.

Also known as

Vermentino, Favorita (same variety).

Good to know

Its name comes from the dialect word 'pigau', meaning freckled, after the rust-coloured spots that appear on the ripe berries.
