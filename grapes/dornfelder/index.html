Dornfelder grape

Red grape

Dornfelder is a red grape known for black cherry, plum, spice. Its best-known homes are Germany.

StyleRed
Body●●●○○ (3/5)
FlavoursBlack cherry, plum, spice
Key regionsGermany

Origin and history

A German crossing bred in 1955 by August Herold at the Weinsberg research institute, from Helfensteiner and Heroldrebe, named after Immanuel Dornfeld, founder of Württemberg's wine school.

Where it grows

Pfalz, Rheinhessen and Württemberg in Germany, with smaller plantings in England, Switzerland and the United States.

Structure

Medium body, deep inky colour, soft tannins and fresh acidity; made both fruity and off-dry, or dry and oak-aged.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: black cherry, plum, blackberry, elderberry and violet. With age: dried plum, spice and vanilla (when oaked).

Ageing

Most drink within 1–4 years; barrique-aged examples can age 5–8 years.

Good to know

Released in 1979, it quickly became Germany's second most planted red after Spätburgunder, thanks to its colour and reliable yields.
