Teroldego grape

Red grape

Teroldego is a red grape known for blackberry, plum, pepper. Its best-known homes are Trentino.

StyleRed
Body●●●●○ (4/5)
FlavoursBlackberry, plum, pepper
Key regionsTrentino

Origin and history

Ancient grape of the Rotaliano plain in Trentino; DNA links it to Dureza, a parent of Syrah, making it a distant relative of Syrah, and it is a parent of Lagrein.

Where it grows

Trentino, above all the Campo Rotaliano (Teroldego Rotaliano DOC), with small plantings in Australia and California.

Structure

Medium to full body, deep colour, lively acidity and moderate, smooth tannins.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: blackberry, black cherry, plum, violet and pepper. With age: tar, leather, liquorice and spice.

Ageing

Most best within 3–7 years; Foradori's finest bottlings can age 10–15.

Good to know

Elisabetta Foradori's work from the 1980s, including her Granato bottling and later amphora-aged wines, rescued Teroldego's reputation.
