Grechetto grape

White grape

Grechetto is a white grape known for pear, almond, citrus. Its best-known homes are Umbria.

StyleWhite
Body●●●○○ (3/5)
FlavoursPear, almond, citrus
Key regionsUmbria

Origin and history

Central Italian grape whose name hints at Greek origins; DNA shows 'Grechetto' covers two different varieties, Grechetto di Orvieto and Grechetto di Todi.

Where it grows

Umbria (Orvieto, Todi, Montefalco, Torgiano, Colli Martani) and northern Lazio; Grechetto di Todi is the same grape as Emilia-Romagna's Pignoletto.

Structure

Medium body, medium acidity and a slightly oily, firm texture thanks to thick skins; usually dry.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: pear, lemon, white flowers and almond. With age: honey, nuts, dried herbs and a gentle waxiness.

Ageing

Typically best within 1–4 years; quality single-varietal Grechetto and Vin Santo can last far longer.

Also known as

Grechetto di Orvieto, Grechetto di Todi (= Pignoletto).

Good to know

It is the backbone of quality Orvieto and its thick skins resist rot, making it ideal for the dried-grape Vin Santo of Umbria.
