Garganega grape

White grape

Garganega is a white grape known for almond, pear, citrus. Its best-known homes are Soave.

StyleWhite
Body●●●○○ (3/5)
FlavoursAlmond, pear, citrus
Key regionsSoave

Origin and history

An ancient grape of the Veneto; DNA shows it is the same as Sicily's Grecanico Dorato and parent of several Italian varieties.

Where it grows

Veneto (Soave, Gambellara, Colli Berici, Bianco di Custoza), and in Sicily as Grecanico.

Structure

Medium body with fresh, moderate to high acidity; dry, or sweet from dried grapes as Recioto di Soave.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: almond, pear, lemon, chamomile and white flowers. With age: honey, marzipan and mineral, saline notes.

Ageing

Most Soave is best within 1–4 years; top Soave Classico can age 10–15 years and Recioto longer.

Also known as

Grecanico Dorato (Sicily).

Good to know

The volcanic hills of Soave Classico give the finest wines; producers such as Pieropan and Inama made single-vineyard Soave famous.
