Chasselas grape

White grape

Chasselas is a white grape known for apple, citrus, mineral. Its best-known homes are Switzerland.

StyleWhite
Body●●○○○ (2/5)
FlavoursApple, citrus, mineral
Key regionsSwitzerland

Origin and history

One of the oldest known grapes, thought to originate around Lake Geneva in Switzerland, where it has been grown for many centuries.

Where it grows

Switzerland (Vaud: Lavaux, Dézaley, Yvorne; Valais as Fendant), Pouilly-sur-Loire, Alsace, Savoie (Crépy) and Germany's Baden (Markgräflerland).

Structure

Light to medium body, low acidity and moderate alcohol; dry and delicate, often with a light spritz.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: apple, pear, lemon, lime blossom and a mineral, stony note. With age: honey, toast, hazelnut and dried flowers.

Ageing

Most within 1–3 years; top Dézaley and Calamin can age for 10–20 years.

Also known as

Fendant (Valais), Gutedel (Germany), Perlan (Geneva).

Good to know

Its steep Lavaux terraces above Lake Geneva became a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2007; it is also a popular table grape.
