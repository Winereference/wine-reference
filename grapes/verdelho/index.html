Verdelho grape

White grape

Verdelho is a white grape known for citrus, tropical, honey. Its best-known homes are Madeira, Australia.

StyleWhite
Body●●●○○ (3/5)
FlavoursCitrus, tropical, honey
Key regionsMadeira, Australia

Origin and history

A Portuguese grape most closely associated with the island of Madeira, where it has been grown for centuries; also traditional in the Azores.

Where it grows

Madeira and the Azores (Pico), plus Australia (Hunter Valley, Western Australia, Langhorne Creek), and small amounts in mainland Portugal and Spain.

Structure

Medium to full body with high acidity; dry or off-dry as table wine, medium-dry when made as fortified Madeira.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: lime, lemon, pineapple, guava and honeysuckle. With age, especially as Madeira: honey, caramel, dried fruit, nuts and smoke.

Ageing

Table wine 2–5 years; Verdelho Madeira can last 50 years or more.

Also known as

Verdelho Branco. Not the same as Spain's Verdejo.

Good to know

It is one of Madeira's four noble grapes, giving the medium-dry style between dry Sercial and sweeter Bual and Malmsey.
