Sauvignon Blanc grape

White grape

Sauvignon Blanc is a white grape known for grapefruit, lime, fresh herbs. Its best-known homes are Loire, Marlborough, Bordeaux.

StyleWhite
Body●●○○○ (2/5)
FlavoursGrapefruit, lime, fresh herbs
Key regionsLoire, Marlborough, Bordeaux
Food pairingGoat cheese, salads, shellfish

Origin and history

From western France (the Loire and Bordeaux); its name means 'wild white', and it is a parent of Cabernet Sauvignon.

Where it grows

Sancerre and Pouilly-Fumé in the Loire, Bordeaux, Marlborough, Casablanca and Leyda in Chile, South Africa, Styria and California.

Structure

Light to medium body, high acidity and very aromatic.

Aromas, young and aged

Gooseberry, cut grass, green pepper, passion fruit, grapefruit and, in the Loire, a flinty, smoky note.

Ageing

Best young and fresh (1–3 years); oaked Bordeaux whites and top Pouilly-Fumé can age longer.

Also known as

Fumé Blanc (oaked style, a name coined by Robert Mondavi in California).

Good to know

Blended with Sémillon it makes both dry white Bordeaux and the sweet wines of Sauternes.
