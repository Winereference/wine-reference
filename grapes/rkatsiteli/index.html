Rkatsiteli grape

White grape

Rkatsiteli is a white grape known for apple, citrus, honey. Its best-known homes are Georgia.

StyleWhite
Body●●●○○ (3/5)
FlavoursApple, citrus, honey
Key regionsGeorgia

Origin and history

One of the oldest grapes in Georgia, from the Kakheti region; its name means 'red stem'.

Where it grows

Georgia (Kakheti), plus Ukraine, Moldova, Bulgaria, Armenia, Russia and the Finger Lakes in New York.

Structure

Medium to full body with high acidity; dry, from crisp and fresh to amber, tannic skin-contact styles.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: green apple, quince, lemon and white flowers. With age or skin contact: honey, dried fruit, nuts and tea.

Ageing

Fresh styles are best within 1–3 years; qvevri amber wines can age 10+ years.

Also known as

Rkaziteli.

Good to know

Fermented and aged on its skins in buried clay qvevri, it makes Georgia's famous amber wines, a method on UNESCO's heritage list.
