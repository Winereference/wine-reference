Zinfandel grape

Photo: Hari Nandakumar on Unsplash

Red grape

Zinfandel is a red grape known for blackberry, plum, pepper. Its best-known homes are California.

StyleRed
Body●●●●○ (4/5)
FlavoursBlackberry, plum, pepper
Key regionsCalifornia

Origin and history

DNA shows it is the same vine as Croatia's Tribidrag (Crljenak Kaštelanski) and Italy's Primitivo; it reached the US in the 1820s and spread through California.

Where it grows

California (Sonoma's Dry Creek Valley, Lodi, Paso Robles, Amador County, Napa), plus Croatia and Puglia under other names, and Western Australia.

Structure

Full body, high alcohol (often 14.5–16%), moderate acidity and medium tannins; berries ripen unevenly in the bunch.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: blackberry, raspberry jam, plum, black pepper and sweet spice. With age: dried fruit, raisin, leather and earth.

Ageing

Most best within 2–6 years; top old-vine examples, such as Ridge's, can age 10–20.

Also known as

Primitivo, Tribidrag, Crljenak Kaštelanski.

Good to know

Sweet pink White Zinfandel became a hit after a stuck fermentation at Sutter Home in 1975; its popularity helped save many old Zinfandel vineyards.
