Savagnin grape

White grape

Savagnin is a white grape known for apple, walnut, spice. Its best-known homes are Jura.

StyleWhite
Body●●●○○ (3/5)
FlavoursApple, walnut, spice
Key regionsJura

Origin and history

An ancient variety from the region between north-east France and southern Germany; DNA shows it is a parent of many grapes, including Grüner Veltliner and Silvaner.

Where it grows

The Jura (Château-Chalon, Arbois, L'Étoile, Côtes du Jura), Switzerland's Valais, Germany, Austria and Australia.

Structure

Medium to full body with high acidity; dry, made either topped-up (ouillé) or aged oxidatively under a veil of yeast.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: apple, citrus, quince and spice. With age and flor (Vin Jaune): walnut, curry spice, mushroom and toasted nuts.

Ageing

Fresh ouillé styles last 5–10 years; Vin Jaune can age for 50 years or more.

Also known as

Traminer, Naturé (Jura), Heida or Païen (Valais).

Good to know

Vin Jaune is aged at least six years and three months in barrel under a yeast film and sold in the 62cl clavelin bottle.
