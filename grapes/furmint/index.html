Furmint grape

White grape

Furmint is a white grape known for apple, pear, honey. Its best-known homes are Tokaj.

StyleWhite
Body●●●○○ (3/5)
FlavoursApple, pear, honey
Key regionsTokaj

Origin and history

A Hungarian grape long tied to Tokaj, recorded there since at least the 17th century.

Where it grows

Hungary (Tokaj, Somló), Slovakia's Tokaj area, Slovenia (Štajerska), Austria (Rust in Burgenland) and Croatia.

Structure

Medium to full body with very high acidity; made bone dry or as Tokaji Aszú and Eszencia sweet wines.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: green apple, pear, lime, smoke and mineral notes. With age: honey, quince, apricot, marmalade and walnut.

Ageing

Dry Furmint ages 5–15 years; Tokaji Aszú can last 50 years and Eszencia for a century or more.

Also known as

Šipon (Slovenia), Zapfner (Austria), Moslavac (Croatia).

Good to know

Its thin skins make it very prone to botrytis, the key to Tokaji Aszú, once celebrated as 'the wine of kings, the king of wines'.
