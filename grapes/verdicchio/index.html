Verdicchio grape

White grape

Verdicchio is a white grape known for lemon, almond, herbs. Its best-known homes are Marche.

StyleWhite
Body●●●○○ (3/5)
FlavoursLemon, almond, herbs
Key regionsMarche

Origin and history

A native grape of the Marche, recorded there since the 16th century; DNA shows it is identical to Trebbiano di Soave and Turbiana.

Where it grows

Marche (Castelli di Jesi, Matelica) and, as Turbiana, in Lugana on Lake Garda.

Structure

Medium body with high acidity; dry, usually unoaked, also sparkling and passito.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: lemon, green apple, almond, fennel and herbs. With age: honey, almond paste and saline, mineral notes.

Ageing

Most is best within 2–4 years; Castelli di Jesi Riserva can age 10–15 years.

Also known as

Trebbiano di Soave, Turbiana, Trebbiano di Lugana.

Good to know

It became famous in the 1950s thanks to Fazi Battaglia's curvy amphora-shaped bottle, and has a characteristic bitter-almond finish.
