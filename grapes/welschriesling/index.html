Welschriesling grape

White grape

Welschriesling is a white grape known for apple, citrus, herbs. Its best-known homes are Austria.

StyleWhite
Body●●○○○ (2/5)
FlavoursApple, citrus, herbs
Key regionsAustria

Origin and history

Of uncertain origin, probably the Danube region; despite the name it is unrelated to Riesling.

Where it grows

Austria (Burgenland, Styria), Croatia (Slavonia), Hungary (Lake Balaton), Slovenia, Serbia, the Czech Republic and northern Italy.

Structure

Light to medium body with crisp, high acidity; dry and fresh, or intensely sweet when botrytised.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: green apple, lemon, herbs and white flowers. Sweet styles: apricot, honey and candied citrus with age.

Ageing

Dry styles are best within 1–3 years; Burgenland Trockenbeerenauslese can age 20+ years.

Also known as

Graševina (Croatia), Olaszrizling (Hungary), Laški Rizling (Slovenia), Riesling Italico (Italy), Ryzlink Vlašský (Czech Republic).

Good to know

Croatia's most planted grape, and the base of sweet wines from the botrytis-prone shores of Lake Neusiedl in Burgenland.
