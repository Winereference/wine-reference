Schiava grape

Red grape

Schiava is a red grape known for red cherry, strawberry, almond. Its best-known homes are Alto Adige.

StyleRed
Body●○○○○ (1/5)
FlavoursRed cherry, strawberry, almond
Key regionsAlto Adige

Origin and history

An old family of grapes from the Alpine foothills, probably named after Slavic workers ('schiavi'); it has been grown in Alto Adige for centuries.

Where it grows

Alto Adige and Trentino in Italy, and Württemberg in Germany (as Trollinger).

Structure

Light body, pale colour, soft low tannins and gentle acidity: one of the lightest reds made.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: red cherry, strawberry, violet, bitter almond and cotton candy. With age: mostly best fresh, gaining dried cherry and almond.

Ageing

Drink within 1–3 years; old-vine Santa Maddalena can hold for 5–8 years.

Also known as

Vernatsch (South Tyrol), Trollinger (Germany), Black Hamburg (UK, as table grape).

Good to know

The Great Vine at Hampton Court, planted in 1768 and among the world's largest, is Schiava Grossa, grown as the table grape Black Hamburg.
