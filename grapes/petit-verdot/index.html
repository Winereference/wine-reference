Petit Verdot grape

Red grape

Petit Verdot is a red grape known for blackberry, violet, spice. Its best-known homes are Bordeaux, Australia.

StyleRed
Body●●●●● (5/5)
FlavoursBlackberry, violet, spice
Key regionsBordeaux, Australia

Origin and history

An old Bordeaux variety, recorded there since at least the 18th century; it ripens so late that Bordeaux growers use it only in small amounts.

Where it grows

Bordeaux (Médoc), Spain, Australia (Riverina, McLaren Vale), Virginia, California, Washington, Chile and Argentina.

Structure

Full body, very deep colour, high acidity and high, firm tannins.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: blackberry, blueberry, violet, sage and spice. With age: leather, cedar, tobacco and dried black fruit.

Ageing

Varietal wines best within 3–10 years; as part of top Bordeaux blends, it helps them age 20 years or more.

Good to know

In the Médoc it usually makes up only 2–5% of a blend, adding colour, tannin and spice, but in warm climates it ripens fully and is bottled on its own.
