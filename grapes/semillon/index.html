Sémillon grape

White grape

Sémillon is a white grape known for lemon, honey, wax. Its best-known homes are Bordeaux, Hunter Valley.

StyleWhite
Body●●●●○ (4/5)
FlavoursLemon, honey, wax
Key regionsBordeaux, Hunter Valley

Origin and history

From Bordeaux, probably the Sauternes area, first recorded in the 18th century.

Where it grows

Bordeaux (Sauternes, Barsac, Graves, Pessac-Léognan), Australia's Hunter Valley, Barossa and Margaret River, South Africa and Washington State.

Structure

Medium to full body, low to moderate acidity and a waxy, oily texture; dry or, when botrytised, intensely sweet.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: lemon, lime, grass and green apple. With age: honey, toast, lanolin, beeswax and (in sweet wines) apricot and marmalade.

Ageing

Hunter Valley Semillon needs 5–10 years and can go 20+; top Sauternes such as Château d'Yquem can last over a century.

Also known as

Semillon, Groendruif (historic, South Africa), Hunter River Riesling (historic, Australia).

Good to know

Unoaked, low-alcohol Hunter Semillon develops toasty, honeyed notes in bottle that seem like oak but come purely from age.
