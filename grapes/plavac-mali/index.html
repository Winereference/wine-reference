Plavac Mali grape

Red grape

Plavac Mali is a red grape known for blackberry, plum, herbs. Its best-known homes are Croatia.

StyleRed
Body●●●●● (5/5)
FlavoursBlackberry, plum, herbs
Key regionsCroatia

Origin and history

Native to Dalmatia in Croatia; DNA shows it is the offspring of Tribidrag (Zinfandel, locally Crljenak Kaštelanski) and Dobričić.

Where it grows

Dalmatia's coast and islands, especially the Pelješac peninsula (Dingač, Postup), Hvar, Brač, Korčula and Vis.

Structure

Full body, high alcohol, firm tannins and moderate acidity, often with a rustic, powerful style.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: blackberry, plum, carob, black pepper and wild herbs. With age: dried fig, prune, leather, tobacco and earth.

Ageing

Most drink within 3–6 years; top Dingač can age 10–15 years.

Also known as

Plavac, Pagadebit Crni.

Good to know

Dingač became Croatia's first protected appellation in 1961; its vines cling to steep, sun-baked slopes running down to the Adriatic.
