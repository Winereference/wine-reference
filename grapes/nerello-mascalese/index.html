Nerello Mascalese grape

Red grape

Nerello Mascalese is a red grape known for red cherry, herbs, smoke. Its best-known homes are Etna, Sicily.

StyleRed
Body●●●○○ (3/5)
FlavoursRed cherry, herbs, smoke
Key regionsEtna, Sicily

Origin and history

A Sicilian variety named after the Mascali plain below Mount Etna; DNA shows it is a natural cross of Sangiovese and Mantonico Bianco.

Where it grows

Mount Etna (Etna Rosso DOC) and the Messina area (Faro DOC), often blended with Nerello Cappuccio.

Structure

Pale colour, medium body, high acidity and fine but firm tannins: often likened to Pinot Noir or Nebbiolo.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: red cherry, raspberry, wild herbs, orange peel and volcanic smoke or ash. With age: dried cherry, tea leaf, earth and spice.

Ageing

Most best within 3–8 years; top single-contrada Etna wines can age 15–20.

Also known as

Nerello, Niureddu Mascalese.

Good to know

Many Etna vines are ungrafted, pre-phylloxera bush vines up to 100 years old, grown at 400–1,000 metres on volcanic soils, with wines labelled by 'contrada'.
