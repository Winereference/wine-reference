Arneis grape

White grape

Arneis is a white grape known for pear, peach, almond. Its best-known homes are Piedmont.

StyleWhite
Body●●●○○ (3/5)
FlavoursPear, peach, almond
Key regionsPiedmont

Origin and history

An old Piedmontese variety from the Roero hills; its name means 'little rascal' in local dialect, a nod to how tricky it is to grow.

Where it grows

Roero (Roero Arneis DOCG) on the left bank of the Tanaro, Langhe DOC, and small plantings in Liguria, Australia and California.

Structure

Medium body, soft medium acidity and a round, slightly creamy texture; dry and usually unoaked.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: pear, white peach, apricot, chamomile and a bitter-almond finish. With age: honey, hazelnut and dried herbs.

Ageing

Drink within 1–3 years; top Roero Riserva can develop for around 5–7 years.

Also known as

Bianchetta, Nebbiolo Bianco.

Good to know

Once blended into Nebbiolo to soften it, Arneis almost vanished by the 1970s until producers such as Vietti and Bruno Giacosa revived it as a varietal wine.
