Cabernet Sauvignon grape

Red grape

Cabernet Sauvignon is a red grape known for blackcurrant, cedar, graphite. Its best-known homes are Bordeaux, Napa Valley, Coonawarra.

StyleRed
Body●●●●● (5/5)
FlavoursBlackcurrant, cedar, graphite
Key regionsBordeaux, Napa Valley, Coonawarra
Food pairingSteak, lamb, aged cheddar

Origin and history

Born in Bordeaux in the 1600s as a natural cross of Cabernet Franc and Sauvignon Blanc, proven by DNA tests at UC Davis in 1997.

Where it grows

Bordeaux's Left Bank (Médoc, Pauillac, Margaux), Napa Valley, Coonawarra and Margaret River, Maipo Valley, Bolgheri, Washington State and Stellenbosch.

Structure

Full body, high tannin, medium-plus acidity. Small, thick-skinned berries give deep colour and grip.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: blackcurrant, black cherry, mint, green pepper and cedar from oak. With age: cigar box, tobacco, pencil shavings and dried herbs.

Ageing

Everyday bottles drink well at 3–8 years; top Bordeaux and Napa wines can age 20–40 years.

Also known as

Cab, Cab Sauv.

Good to know

Often blended with Merlot and Cabernet Franc (the Bordeaux blend) to soften its tannins.
