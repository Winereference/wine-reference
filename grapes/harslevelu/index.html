Hárslevelű grape

White grape

Hárslevelű is a white grape known for pear, peach, honey. Its best-known homes are Tokaj.

StyleWhite
Body●●●○○ (3/5)
FlavoursPear, peach, honey
Key regionsTokaj

Origin and history

A Hungarian grape, recorded in Tokaj since the 18th century; its name means 'linden leaf'.

Where it grows

Hungary (Tokaj, Eger's Debrő, Somló, Villány), Slovakia and a little in South Africa.

Structure

Medium to full body with moderate to high acidity; dry, off-dry or sweet, with a softer, rounder feel than Furmint.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: pear, peach, linden blossom, smoke and spice. With age: honey, apricot, beeswax and nuts.

Ageing

Dry wines last 3–10 years; in Tokaji Aszú blends it ages for decades.

Also known as

Lipovina (Slovakia), Lindenblättriger.

Good to know

Tokaj's second grape, it adds perfume and roundness to Furmint's acidity in Tokaji Aszú.
