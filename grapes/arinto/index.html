Arinto grape

White grape

Arinto is a white grape known for citrus, apple, mineral. Its best-known homes are Portugal.

StyleWhite
Body●●○○○ (2/5)
FlavoursCitrus, apple, mineral
Key regionsPortugal

Origin and history

An old Portuguese grape, grown in Bucelas north of Lisbon for centuries.

Where it grows

Bucelas, Lisboa, Tejo, Alentejo and Vinho Verde (as Pedernã), plus Bairrada and Dão.

Structure

Light to medium body, high acidity and dry, keeping its freshness even in hot climates.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: lemon, green apple, citrus peel and stone. With age: honey, beeswax, nuts and petrol notes.

Ageing

Most drink within 1–4 years; top Bucelas can age 10–15 years.

Also known as

Pedernã (Vinho Verde), Arinto de Bucelas.

Good to know

Bucelas wines must be at least 75% Arinto; 19th-century English buyers called its wine 'Lisbon hock'.
