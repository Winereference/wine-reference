Encruzado grape

White grape

Encruzado is a white grape known for citrus, pear, mineral. Its best-known homes are Dão.

StyleWhite
Body●●●○○ (3/5)
FlavoursCitrus, pear, mineral
Key regionsDão

Origin and history

A native Portuguese grape from the Dão, long used as a blending component before growers began bottling it on its own from the 1990s.

Where it grows

Almost entirely in the Dão, central Portugal, on granite soils between the Serra da Estrela and Caramulo mountains; tiny plantings elsewhere in Portugal.

Structure

Medium to full body with fresh, firm acidity; dry, often barrel-fermented, giving a creamy, Burgundian texture.

Aromas, young and aged

Young: lemon, pear, white flowers and a flinty, mineral edge. With age: hazelnut, beeswax, toast and resinous, savoury notes.

Ageing

Good examples improve for 5–10 years, and the best barrel-fermented wines can last 15 years or more.

Good to know

Often called Portugal's answer to white Burgundy; it resists oxidation well, which suits barrel fermentation and lees ageing.
