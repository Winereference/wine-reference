About Rutherglen

Hot north-east Victorian town on the Murray River, famous for richly aged fortified Muscat and Topaque. Hot continental climate with long, dry and sunny autumns, which let Muscat and Muscadelle shrivel on the vine and build very high sugar. Cold winter nights; frost is a risk on lower ground. Soils are mostly red loams over clay, with sandier patches along the Murray.

Rutherglen: key grapes Muscat à Petits Grains Rouge, Muscadelle, Durif, Shiraz.

The grape

  • Muscat (white): floral, peach, grape. Best known in Alsace, Italy.

More food ideas

  • Tiramisu: Light, sweet, low-alcohol Moscato d'Asti refreshes after mascarpone, while raisiny Pedro Ximénez sherry echoes the coffee and cocoa.
  • Crème brûlée: Sauternes and botrytised Sémillon bring honey, apricot and acidity that match vanilla custard and caramelised sugar.
  • Sticky toffee pudding: Dates and toffee sauce need an even sweeter wine; PX sherry, Rutherglen Muscat or Tokaji Aszú match the caramel and dried-fruit notes.
  • Lemon tart: A tangy lemon curd tart needs a sweet wine with high acidity, otherwise the dessert makes the wine taste flat.
  • Bakewell tart: Honeyed sweet wines echo the frangipane's almond and the jam's fruit, and must be at least as sweet as the tart.
