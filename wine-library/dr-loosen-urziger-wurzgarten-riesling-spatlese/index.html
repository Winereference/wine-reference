About Mosel

Steep slate slopes, light and racy. Cool climate with very steep slate slopes above a winding river.

Mosel: key grapes Riesling.

The grape

  • Riesling (white): peach, lime, petrol, honey. Best known in Mosel, Alsace, Finger Lakes.

More food ideas

  • Spicy curry: A touch of sweetness and low alcohol in off-dry Riesling cool chilli heat, while fruity rosé handles aromatic spice without turning bitter.
  • Cheese board: Mixed cheeses need a mixed line-up: Riesling for soft and blue cheeses, soft Merlot for hard cheeses, and Prosecco's bubbles to refresh.
  • Pad Thai: Sweet, sour, salty and spicy flavours suit aromatic whites; Riesling's lime acidity and Gewürztraminer's lychee match tamarind and peanuts.
  • Thai green curry: Coconut, lemongrass and green chilli suit off-dry, aromatic whites whose touch of sweetness tames heat and echoes the herbs.
  • Kung pao chicken: Sichuan pepper, chilli and peanuts need fruity, refreshing wines; off-dry Riesling and fuller rosé handle the heat and sweet-sour glaze.
