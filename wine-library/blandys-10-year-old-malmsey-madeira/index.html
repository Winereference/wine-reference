About Madeira

Steep volcanic Atlantic island far off Morocco, whose heat-aged, oxidative fortified wines are close to indestructible. Subtropical and humid, with mild winters and plenty of rain, especially on the cooler north side. Vines grow on narrow hand-built terraces (poios) cut into basalt and other volcanic soils, often trained on low pergolas, in tiny plots that make every harvest labour-intensive.

Madeira: key grapes Tinta Negra, Sercial, Verdelho, Boal, Malvasia, Terrantez.

The grape

  • Malvasia (white): peach, floral, almond. Best known in Italy, Iberia.

More food ideas

  • Christmas pudding: Dried fruit, spice and brandy demand an intensely sweet, nutty wine; Malmsey Madeira, PX sherry or Rutherglen Muscat match its richness.
  • Mince pies: Tawny Port, Muscat or Malmsey are sweeter than the mincemeat and echo its dried fruit, peel and spice, a classic Christmas sideboard match.
