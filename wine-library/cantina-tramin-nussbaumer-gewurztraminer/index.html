About Trentino-Alto Adige

Crisp Alpine whites and bright reds. Alpine valleys with big day-night temperature swings and cool nights that keep acidity high.

Trentino-Alto Adige: key grapes Pinot Grigio, Gewürztraminer, Lagrein, Teroldego.

The grape

  • Gewürztraminer (white): lychee, rose, spice. Best known in Alsace.

More food ideas

  • Pad Thai: Sweet, sour, salty and spicy flavours suit aromatic whites; Riesling's lime acidity and Gewürztraminer's lychee match tamarind and peanuts.
  • Thai green curry: Coconut, lemongrass and green chilli suit off-dry, aromatic whites whose touch of sweetness tames heat and echoes the herbs.
  • Crispy duck pancakes: Pinot Noir's cherry fruit matches sweet hoisin and rich duck, while spicy Gewürztraminer picks up five-spice and plum.
  • Chicken tikka masala: Aromatic, off-dry whites soothe the spice and echo the creamy, fragrant sauce; high tannins would only amplify the heat.
  • Beef rendang: Rich coconut, lemongrass and slow-cooked beef can take a ripe, fruity red, while spicy Gewürztraminer mirrors the aromatics.
