About Bekaa Valley

High plateau between Lebanon's two mountain ranges and the heart of its wine industry. Vineyards sit at roughly 900 to 1,100 m on a plateau between the Mount Lebanon and Anti-Lebanon ranges, which block most sea air and rain. Summers are hot, dry and sunny with cool nights; winters bring rain and sometimes snow. Soils are mostly limestone, clay and gravel.

Bekaa Valley: key grapes Cabernet Sauvignon, Cinsault, Syrah, Carignan, Merlot, Obaideh, Merwah.

The grapes

  • Cabernet Sauvignon (red): blackcurrant, cedar, graphite. Best known in Bordeaux, Napa Valley, Coonawarra.
  • Cinsault (red): red cherry, strawberry, floral. Best known in Languedoc, South Africa.
  • Carignan (red): blackberry, cherry, herbs. Best known in Languedoc, Sardinia.

More food ideas

  • Steak: Firm tannins in Cabernet and Tempranillo cut through the fat and char of a seared steak, while peppery Shiraz echoes the sauce.
  • Roast leg of lamb: Lamb's rich fat and rosemary call for structured reds; Cabernet's cassis and cedar and Rioja's savoury oak are the classic partners.
  • Lamb tagine with apricots: Warm spices, apricots and lamb suit ripe, spicy Grenache and Syrah blends, whose soft fruit embraces the sweetness.
  • Beef Wellington: Tender fillet and mushroom duxelles suit a structured but polished red; Bordeaux or Burgundy matches the richness without overpowering the pastry.
  • Steak and ale pie: Structured, savoury reds stand up to slow-braised beef and the malty depth of an ale gravy under buttery pastry.
