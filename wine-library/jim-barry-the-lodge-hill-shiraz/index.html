About Clare Valley

Upland valleys north of Adelaide, famous for bone-dry, lime-scented Riesling. Continental, with hot days and cold nights at 300–500 m in the northern Mount Lofty Ranges; afternoon breezes and the night-time chill keep acidity in the grapes. Soils change from valley to valley: red loam over limestone around Watervale, hard slate and shale at Polish Hill River, and sandier ground further north.

Clare Valley: key grapes Riesling, Shiraz, Cabernet Sauvignon.

The grape

  • Syrah / Shiraz (red): blackberry, pepper, smoke. Best known in Rhône, Barossa, Washington.

More food ideas

  • Steak: Firm tannins in Cabernet and Tempranillo cut through the fat and char of a seared steak, while peppery Shiraz echoes the sauce.
  • Shepherd's pie: Peppery, juicy Grenache and Shiraz match savoury lamb mince and gravy without overwhelming a homely dish.
  • Venison stew: Gamey venison loves Syrah's dark fruit, pepper and smoky notes, and Grenache-led Rhône blends add warmth for a winter stew.
  • Lamb tagine with apricots: Warm spices, apricots and lamb suit ripe, spicy Grenache and Syrah blends, whose soft fruit embraces the sweetness.
  • Pheasant casserole: Earthy, gamey notes in pheasant meet the forest-floor character of mature Pinot Noir or the savoury spice of northern Rhône Syrah.
