About Savoie

Scattered Alpine vineyards between Lake Geneva and the Isère valley: light, crisp mountain whites, peppery Mondeuse reds and a growing range of crémant. Mountain climate softened by the large lakes (Geneva, Le Bourget) and by sheltered, south- and south-east-facing slopes, mostly at 250–500 m. Soils are glacial moraine, limestone scree and marl; Apremont and Abymes sit on debris from the great 1248 landslide of Mont Granier.

Savoie: key grapes Jacquère, Altesse, Bergeron (Roussanne), Chasselas, Gringet, Mondeuse, Gamay.

The grape

  • Roussanne (white): pear, herbs, honey. Best known in Rhône.

More food ideas

  • Tartiflette: Alpine Savoie wines are the classic match: crisp Jacquère cuts through Reblochon and bacon, while peppery Mondeuse suits red-wine lovers.
