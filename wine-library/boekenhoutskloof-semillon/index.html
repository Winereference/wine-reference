About Franschhoek

Mountain-ringed valley famed for old-vine Sémillon and Cap Classique.

Franschhoek: key grapes Chardonnay, Sémillon, Cabernet Sauvignon, Shiraz.

The grape

  • Sémillon (white): lemon, honey, wax. Best known in Bordeaux, Hunter Valley.

More food ideas

  • Crème brûlée: Sauternes and botrytised Sémillon bring honey, apricot and acidity that match vanilla custard and caramelised sugar.
  • Lemon tart: A tangy lemon curd tart needs a sweet wine with high acidity, otherwise the dessert makes the wine taste flat.
  • Apple crumble: Baked apple and buttery crumble chime with the honeyed apricot and orchard-fruit notes of botrytised Sémillon or sweet Chenin.
  • Bakewell tart: Honeyed sweet wines echo the frangipane's almond and the jam's fruit, and must be at least as sweet as the tart.
  • Blue cheese and pear salad: Salty blue cheese and sweet pear love wines with sweetness and acidity; off-dry Riesling or Sauternes mirror the fruit and tame the tang.
