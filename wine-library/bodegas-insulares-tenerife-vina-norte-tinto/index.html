About Tacoronte-Acentejo

The oldest and largest DO on Tenerife, on the island's green north coast. Humid, mild north-facing slopes cooled by trade winds, on volcanic soils.

Tacoronte-Acentejo: key grapes Listán Negro, Negramoll.

The grape

  • Listán Negro (red): red cherry, pepper, smoke, herbs. Best known in Tenerife, Lanzarote.
