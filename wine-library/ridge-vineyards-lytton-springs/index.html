The grapes

  • Zinfandel (red): blackberry, plum, pepper. Best known in California.
  • Petite Sirah (red): blackberry, plum, pepper. Best known in California.

More food ideas

  • Chilli con carne: Ripe, plush, low-tannin reds like Malbec and Zinfandel soften chilli heat and match the dish's smoky spice and beans.
  • Beef rendang: Rich coconut, lemongrass and slow-cooked beef can take a ripe, fruity red, while spicy Gewürztraminer mirrors the aromatics.
  • Beef bulgogi: Ripe, fruity reds and off-dry Riesling echo the sweet soy-and-pear marinade and soften the garlic and sesame intensity.
  • Jerk chicken: Scotch bonnet heat and allspice call for aromatic whites with a touch of sweetness, or a juicy low-tannin red.
  • Pulled pork: Smoky, sweet barbecue flavours want ripe, juicy reds; Zinfandel and Shiraz bring jammy fruit and spice without heavy tannin.
