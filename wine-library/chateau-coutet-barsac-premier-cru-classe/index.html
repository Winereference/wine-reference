About Bordeaux

Structured reds and sweet Sauternes. Maritime climate. Gravel soils on the Left Bank, clay and limestone on the Right Bank.

Bordeaux: key grapes Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Sémillon.

The grapes

  • Sémillon (white): lemon, honey, wax. Best known in Bordeaux, Hunter Valley.
  • Sauvignon Blanc (white): grapefruit, lime, fresh herbs. Best known in Loire, Marlborough, Bordeaux.
  • Muscadelle (white): honeysuckle, grape, citrus, floral. Best known in Bordeaux, Bergerac, Rutherglen.

More food ideas

  • Oysters: Briny oysters love crisp, mineral whites: Champagne's bubbles, Muscadet's saline edge and Sancerre's citrus all act like a squeeze of lemon.
  • Chicken Kiev: Garlic butter and crisp crumbs want a fresh white; Chablis-style Chardonnay or zesty Sauvignon Blanc cut the richness and match the herbs.
  • Fish and chips: Crisp acidity and bubbles slice through batter like a squeeze of lemon, which is why fizz with fish and chips is a British favourite.
  • Moules marinières: Mussels cooked in white wine, shallots and parsley suit light, saline Loire whites, especially Muscadet from near the Atlantic coast.
  • Prawn cocktail: Zesty Sauvignon Blanc or brut Champagne refresh the palate after creamy Marie Rose sauce and complement sweet prawns.
