About Swartland

Dry-farmed old bush vines and South Africa's new wave of natural-leaning winemakers. Hot, dry Mediterranean summers north of Cape Town, mostly farmed without irrigation. Soils swing between decomposed granite (Paardeberg), Malmesbury shale and iron-rich ferricrete called koffieklip.

Swartland: key grapes Chenin Blanc, Syrah, Grenache, Cinsault.

The grape

  • Chenin Blanc (white): apple, quince, honey. Best known in Loire, South Africa.

More food ideas

  • Fish pie: Creamy sauce and buttery mash need a rounded white with fresh acidity, such as unoaked Burgundy Chardonnay or Chenin Blanc.
  • Warm goat's cheese salad: Goat's cheese and Loire Sauvignon Blanc are a classic match: the wine's tangy acidity and herbal notes mirror the cheese's freshness.
  • Slow-roast pork belly: Bright acidity in Riesling or Chenin slices through fatty pork and crackling, while a touch of fruit sweetness flatters the meat.
  • Apple crumble: Baked apple and buttery crumble chime with the honeyed apricot and orchard-fruit notes of botrytised Sémillon or sweet Chenin.
  • Tarragon chicken: Creamy, herb-scented sauces suit rounded whites with fresh acidity, which echo the cream while keeping the anise notes lively.
