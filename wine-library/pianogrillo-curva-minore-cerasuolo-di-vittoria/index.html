About Sicily

Volcanic and Mediterranean styles. Hot and dry Mediterranean climate. Volcanic soils around Mount Etna, with cooler high-altitude vineyards.

Sicily: key grapes Nero d'Avola, Etna Rosso, Grillo.

The grapes

  • Nero d'Avola (red): plum, blackberry, pepper. Best known in Sicily.
  • Frappato (red): strawberry, red cherry, floral. Best known in Sicily.

More food ideas

  • Aubergine parmigiana: Southern Italian reds with ripe fruit and fresh acidity stand up to tomato, aubergine and melted cheese without heavy tannin.
  • Arancini: Crisp-fried Sicilian rice balls with molten cheese love fresh bubbles or a Sicilian white or soft red from their home island.
  • Spaghetti puttanesca: Briny olives, capers and anchovy need high-acid Italian wines; Sangiovese matches the tomato while Verdicchio handles the salty edge.
  • Pasta alla Norma: Sicily's aubergine, tomato and ricotta salata pasta is best with juicy Sicilian Nero d'Avola or a dry island rosé.
  • Caponata: Sicilian reds with ripe cherry fruit and fresh acidity handle caponata's sweet-and-sour agrodolce and soft aubergine.
