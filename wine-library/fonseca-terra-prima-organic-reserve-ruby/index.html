About Douro Valley

Port and robust dry reds. Hot, dry summers and steep schist terraces above the Douro River. The world's oldest demarcated wine region (1756).

Douro Valley: key grapes Touriga Nacional, Touriga Franca.

The grapes

  • Touriga Nacional (red): blackberry, violet, spice. Best known in Douro, Dão.
  • Touriga Franca (red): black cherry, blackberry, violet, spice. Best known in Douro.

More food ideas

  • Chocolate fondant: Chocolate overwhelms most dry wines, so choose a sweet fortified red; ruby or LBV Port has the sweetness and dark fruit to match.
  • Chocolate brownies: Dense, fudgy chocolate overwhelms dry wines; ruby Port or sticky PX sherry are sweet and rich enough to stand up to it.
  • Peri-peri chicken: Fiery, garlicky chicken with Portuguese and southern African roots matches chilled rosé, fruity Douro red or ripe Cape Chenin.
  • Chocolate mousse: Dark chocolate needs a fortified wine at least as sweet and rich, such as ruby Port's dark berries or raisiny PX.
  • Pecan pie: Tawny Port's nutty, caramel notes echo the toasted pecans and syrup, while botrytised Semillon adds honeyed acidity.
