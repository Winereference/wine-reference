About Colonia

A small, mild region by the river. Mild climate close to the Río de la Plata.

Colonia: key grapes Tannat, Albariño.

The grape

  • Tannat (red): blackberry, plum, spice. Best known in Uruguay, Madiran.

More food ideas

  • Cassoulet: Firm, tannic South-West French reds cut through duck fat, sausage and beans, the same pairing locals drink in Toulouse and Castelnaudary.
  • Duck confit: South-west France's tannic Cahors and Madiran reds are the local match, cutting through the rich, salty, crisp-skinned duck.
