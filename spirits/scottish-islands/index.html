Scottish Islands: Scotch malt whisky

Photo: v2osk on Unsplash

Whisky · Scotland

An informal grouping (officially part of the Highlands) covering Skye, Orkney, Jura, Arran and Mull.

SpiritScotch malt whisky
Made fromMalted barley
TasteMaritime and often lightly smoky: sea spray, pepper, heather and honey.
How to drinkNeat or with a splash of water.

How it's made

Scotch rules apply. Many island malts use some peated barley and age in warehouses close to the sea.

Producers

  • Talisker
  • Highland Park
  • Isle of Jura
  • Arran
  • Tobermory

Bottles to try

Whisky · Scotland

Talisker 10 Year Old

Peppery and maritime: smoke, sea spray, dried fruit and chilli warmth.

Serve: Neat or in a highball

Whisky · Scotland

Highland Park 12 Year Old Viking Honour

Heather honey, gentle smoke, orange peel and dried fruit.

Strength: 40% · Age and cask: 12 years, sherry-seasoned oak casks

From Kirkwall on Orkney, founded in 1798. Part of its barley is still malted on traditional floors and dried with local Orkney peat, which is rich in heather because the islands have few trees.

Serve: Neat

Whisky · Scotland

Arran 10 Year Old

Fresh and fruity: apple, citrus, vanilla and a light malty sweetness.

Strength: 46% · Age and cask: 10 years, ex-bourbon and sherry casks

Lochranza distillery opened in 1995, the first legal distillery on the Isle of Arran for over 150 years. The 10 Year Old is bottled without chill-filtering and with natural colour.

Serve: Neat or with a splash of water

Whisky · Scotland

Ledaig 10 Year Old

Peat smoke and sea spray with pepper, lemon and a sweet malty core.

Strength: 46.3% · Age and cask: 10 years, mainly ex-bourbon casks

Ledaig is the peated whisky made at Tobermory distillery on the Isle of Mull; unpeated spirit from the same stills is sold as Tobermory. It is bottled without chill filtration.

Serve: Neat, or with a splash of water
