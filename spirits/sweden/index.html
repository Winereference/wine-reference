Sweden: Vodka and aquavit

Vodka · Sweden

Sweden has a long tradition of 'brännvin', clear spirits drunk with food, and is home to Absolut, one of the world's best-known vodkas.

SpiritVodka and aquavit
Made fromWinter wheat (and potatoes for some aquavit)
TasteClean and soft, with light grain sweetness and a peppery finish.
How to drinkIce cold neat, or with tonic or ginger beer

How it's made

Grain is fermented and distilled in continuous column stills to a very pure spirit, then cut with water.

Winter wheat is milled, mashed and fermented, then distilled continuously in tall column stills to a very high, clean strength. It is cut with soft local water before bottling.

History

Swedes distilled 'brännvin' from grain, and later potatoes, from the 1400s, and it became so common that the state took control of production and sales. Absolut was launched in 1979 for export and became a global brand.

Ageing and label terms

Vodka is not aged. Swedish aquavit is usually clear and unaged, flavoured with caraway, dill, fennel or anise; some brands rest briefly in oak.

Styles

Clean wheat vodkas lead, alongside flavoured vodkas and caraway- or dill-led aquavit, the traditional 'snaps' for holiday tables.

Rules and protected names

'Svensk Vodka' and 'Svensk Aquavit' are EU geographical indications and must be made in Sweden. Vodka must be at least 37.5% ABV in the EU.

How to serve and classic cocktails

Serve ice cold in small glasses with food, or mix: classic cocktails include the Cosmopolitan, Moscow Mule and Vodka Martini.

With food

Pickled herring, gravlax, crispbread with cheese, crayfish at August parties and the Christmas smörgåsbord.

Good to know

At Midsummer and Christmas, Swedes sing short drinking songs, 'snapsvisor', before each glass, starting with 'Helan går'.

Bottles to try

Vodka · Sweden

Absolut Vodka

Clean and full: soft grain sweetness, a hint of dried fruit and a peppery finish.

Strength: 40% · Age and cask: Unaged

Made in and around Åhus in southern Sweden from local winter wheat and well water, using continuous distillation. Launched in 1979, it takes its name from Lars Olsson Smith's 'absolutely pure' vodka of 1879.

Serve: Ice cold, in a Cosmopolitan or Moscow Mule
