Jerez: Brandy de Jerez

Brandy · Spain

Brandy matured in the sherry bodegas of Jerez, picking up rich flavours from the casks.

SpiritBrandy de Jerez
Made fromGrape spirit, mostly from Airén
TasteRaisin, toffee, walnut and sweet spice.
How to drinkNeat after dinner, or with coffee.

How it's made

Aged in ex-sherry casks using the solera system. Categories are Solera, Solera Reserva and Solera Gran Reserva.

Wine, mostly from Airén grapes grown in La Mancha and other parts of Spain, is distilled into wine spirits. Holandas are distilled to under 70% abv in pot stills and keep the most flavour; stronger column-distilled spirits are also used.

History

Spanish brandy grew from wine spirits shipped from Jerez to Dutch traders, who called them 'holandas'. Pedro Domecq launched Fundador in 1874, one of the first branded brandies of Jerez.

Ageing and label terms

Ageing takes place in American oak casks seasoned with sherry, using the solera system. Solera: at least 6 months; Solera Reserva: at least 1 year; Solera Gran Reserva: at least 3 years, in practice often much longer.

Styles

Light, fruity Solera brandies for mixing, and rich, dark Solera Reserva and Gran Reserva brandies with raisin and walnut notes from Oloroso or Pedro Ximénez casks.

Rules and protected names

Brandy de Jerez is a protected geographical indication. It must be aged in the sherry triangle of Jerez de la Frontera, El Puerto de Santa María and Sanlúcar de Barrameda, and bottled at least 36% abv.

How to serve and classic cocktails

Neat in a small glass after dinner. In Spain it is mixed with cola or coffee (carajillo), and it makes a rich Sidecar or a brandy Old Fashioned.

With food

Dark chocolate, turrón, almond cakes, aged Manchego and slow-cooked oxtail.

Good to know

The spirit is distilled elsewhere in Spain, but only brandies aged in the Jerez area may use the Brandy de Jerez name.

Producers

  • Cardenal Mendoza (Sánchez Romate)
  • Lepanto (González Byass)
  • Gran Duque d'Alba
  • Fundador

Bottles to try

Brandy · Spain

Sánchez Romate Cardenal Mendoza Solera Gran Reserva

Raisins, figs, toffee, coffee and sweet spice.

Strength: 40% · Age and cask: Solera Gran Reserva, casks seasoned with oloroso and Pedro Ximénez sherry

A classic Brandy de Jerez from Sánchez Romate, a house founded in 1781. It ages in a solera system of sherry-seasoned American oak butts, which gives its dark, rich, sweet style.

Serve: Neat, or with coffee after dinner

Brandy · Spain

González Byass Lepanto Solera Gran Reserva

Elegant and dry: dried figs, walnuts, vanilla, toffee and a long oloroso finish.

Strength: 36% · Age and cask: Solera Gran Reserva, aged in sherry-seasoned American oak casks

Unusually for Brandy de Jerez, Lepanto is made only from Palomino grapes from Jerez and distilled in copper pot stills. It then passes through a solera system in the González Byass bodegas.

Serve: Neat after dinner
