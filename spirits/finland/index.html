Finland: Finnish vodka and rye spirits

Vodka · Finland

Finland makes clean, pure grain vodka from its own six-row barley, and a new wave of craft distillers uses Finnish rye for gin and whisky.

SpiritFinnish vodka and rye spirits
Made fromBarley, rye and glacial spring water
TasteClean, soft and lightly grainy, with a hint of sweetness; rye spirits are spicier.
How to drinkIce cold and neat, in a vodka tonic, or rye gin with tonic and cucumber.

How it's made

Barley is fermented and distilled in long continuous column runs at Koskenkorva in Ostrobothnia, then cut with soft spring water. Craft distillers such as Kyrö work in copper pot stills with whole-grain rye.

Six-row barley from local farms is fermented and run through a continuous process of many columns at Koskenkorva, giving a very pure spirit, which is cut with spring or glacial water. Kyrö mashes and pot-distils 100% wholegrain rye.

History

Finland's state alcohol company built the Koskenkorva distillery in Ilmajoki in 1938. Finlandia was launched in 1970 and became one of the first global premium vodkas; Kyrö Distillery, founded by friends in a sauna in 2012, revived rye distilling.

Ageing and label terms

Vodka is unaged. Kyrö's rye whisky is aged in new American oak and other casks; their gin is redistilled with botanicals.

Styles

Clean barley vodka, flavoured vodkas, rye gin, rye whisky and Koskenkorva's lower-strength viina.

Rules and protected names

Finnish vodka is a protected geographical indication in the EU: it must be produced in Finland from Finnish ingredients.

How to serve and classic cocktails

Neat and ice cold with food, or in a vodka tonic, Moscow Mule or Gin and Tonic with rye gin.

With food

Smoked and pickled fish, rye bread, crayfish parties and salty liquorice.

Good to know

Salmiakki Koskenkorva, a salty liquorice vodka liqueur, is a Finnish favourite.

Producers

  • Finlandia
  • Koskenkorva
  • Kyrö

Bottles to try

Vodka · Finland

Finlandia Vodka

Clean and soft with light cereal sweetness and a smooth, crisp finish.

Strength: 40% · Age and cask: Unaged

Distilled from Finnish six-row barley at Koskenkorva and blended with glacial spring water at Rajamäki. Launched in 1970, it was one of the first premium vodkas sold worldwide.

Serve: Ice cold in a vodka tonic or Moscow Mule

Vodka · Finland

Koskenkorva Vodka

Pure, slightly sweet grain with a fresh, mineral finish.

Strength: 40% · Age and cask: Unaged

From the Koskenkorva distillery in Ilmajoki, built in 1938. Distilled from barley in a continuous run of many columns, with no added sugar, it is Finland's best-known everyday vodka.

Serve: Neat from the freezer with pickled herring

Gin & juniper · Finland

Kyrö Napue Gin

Juniper, sea buckthorn, meadowsweet and cranberry over a spicy rye base.

Strength: 46.3% · Age and cask: Unaged

Kyrö Distillery in Isokyrö distils this gin from 100% wholegrain rye spirit with Finnish botanicals. It won worldwide attention soon after its first release in 2015.

Serve: With tonic, a sprig of rosemary and fresh cranberries

Vodka · Finland

Koskenkorva Viina

Clean grain spirit with a faint sweetness and a soft, smooth finish.

Strength: 38% · Age and cask: Unaged

Known simply as Kossu, Finland's best-known clear spirit has been made from Finnish barley at Koskenkorva in Ilmajoki since 1953. A touch of sugar makes it a viina rather than a vodka.

Serve: Ice cold as a shot, or with a mixer

Whisky · Finland

Kyrö Malt Rye Whisky

Spicy and grainy, with rye bread, toffee, cinnamon and a fresh herbal edge.

Strength: 47.2% · Age and cask: Aged in new American oak

Made entirely from Finnish malted rye at the Kyrö distillery in Isokyrö, Ostrobothnia, a former dairy turned distillery by friends who met in a sauna.

Serve: Neat, or in a rye Old Fashioned

Gin & juniper · Finland

Kyrö Koskue

Warm and spicy: rye bread, juniper, orange peel, black pepper and vanilla oak.

Strength: 42.6% · Age and cask: 6 to 12 weeks in small American oak barrels

A cask-rested version of Kyrö's rye-based Napue gin from Isokyrö in western Finland. The short spell in new oak adds vanilla and spice, so it drinks between a gin and a young whisky.

Serve: Neat over ice, or in a Martinez or Negroni
