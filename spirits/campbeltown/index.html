Campbeltown: Scotch malt whisky

Whisky · Scotland

Once home to dozens of distilleries and called the whisky capital of the world; today only a handful remain.

SpiritScotch malt whisky
Made fromMalted barley
TasteBriny, oily and slightly smoky, with dried fruit and a coastal tang.
How to drinkNeat, with a drop of water.

How it's made

Scotch rules apply. Springbank malts, distils and bottles everything on site, which is rare in Scotland.

Producers

  • Springbank
  • Glen Scotia
  • Glengyle (Kilkerran)

Bottles to try

Whisky · Scotland

Springbank 10 Year Old

Whisky · Scotland

Glen Scotia Double Cask

Toffee, red fruit, vanilla and a faint salty edge.

Strength: 46% · Age and cask: No age statement, first-fill bourbon casks then Pedro Ximénez sherry finish

One of only three working distilleries in Campbeltown, once called the whisky capital of the world. Bottled without chill filtration.
