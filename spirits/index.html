Spirits guide

The great spirit regions of the world, from Islay and Speyside to Cognac, Jalisco and Oaxaca, with 440 bottles to try.

Each region page explains what is made there and from what, how it is distilled and aged, the rules that protect its name, the producers that matter and how the spirit is best enjoyed, neat, with water or in a cocktail. The bottle notes give strength and age where they are known, so you can compare styles side by side.

Start with Cognac, Islay or Jalisco, or jump to the cocktails to see the spirits at work.
