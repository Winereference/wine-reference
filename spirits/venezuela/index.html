Venezuela: Ron de Venezuela

Rum & cane · Venezuela

Venezuela has made rum since colonial haciendas, and its own protected designation guarantees a mellow, well-aged style led by houses such as Santa Teresa and Diplomático.

SpiritRon de Venezuela
Made fromSugar cane molasses and cane honey
TasteRounded and smooth: toffee, cocoa, orange peel, vanilla and dried fruit.
How to drinkAged rums neat or on ice; younger ones in a Cuba Libre or daiquiri.

How it's made

Under the Ron de Venezuela designation, rum must be distilled in Venezuela and aged at least two years in oak casks. Producers use column stills, pot stills and copper kettles and blend the results.

Most rum is fermented from molasses, while some producers also use cane honey. Distillation mixes tall column stills for light spirit with copper pot stills and batch kettles for heavier spirit; blends are built from several styles.

History

Cane came to Venezuela in the 1500s and rum was distilled on haciendas from colonial times. Hacienda Santa Teresa in Aragua dates from 1796, and Destilerías Unidas, home of Diplomático, was founded at the foot of the Andes in 1959.

Ageing and label terms

Venezuelan rum must spend at least two years in oak, usually ex-bourbon casks, and the warm climate speeds ageing. Santa Teresa 1796 uses a solera system. Labels such as Añejo and Reserva show older blends.

Styles

Light white rums for mixing, mellow aged blends and rich, sweeter sipping rums.

Rules and protected names

Ron de Venezuela is a protected designation of origin set up in 2003: rum must be made from Venezuelan cane, distilled in the country and aged at least two years in oak.

How to serve and classic cocktails

Sip the older rums neat or on ice. Younger ones make good Cuba Libres, daiquiris and rum punches.

With food

Goes with arepas, grilled meats, dark chocolate, flan and coffee desserts.

Good to know

Venezuela was one of the first rum countries to protect its name with a formal designation, alongside Martinique and Guatemala.

Bottles to try

Rum & cane · Venezuela

Diplomático Reserva Exclusiva

Rich and sweet: toffee, orange peel, fudge and liquorice.

Strength: 40% · Age and cask: Blend of rums up to 12 years, ex-bourbon casks

The flagship of Destilerías Unidas in Lara state, blended from pot-still and column rums. One of the best-known sipping rums in the world.

Serve: Neat or over ice

Rum & cane · Venezuela

Diplomático Mantuano

Lighter and drier: dried fruit, cinnamon, caramel and oak.

Strength: 40% · Age and cask: Blend aged up to 8 years in oak

Named after the Mantuanos, the colonial elite of Caracas. A more everyday blend from the same distillery as Reserva Exclusiva.

Serve: Over ice or in a rum Old Fashioned

Rum & cane · Venezuela

Santa Teresa 1796

Dry and elegant: dried fruit, cocoa, leather, oak and spice.

Strength: 40% · Age and cask: Solera-aged, ex-bourbon casks

Made at Hacienda Santa Teresa in Aragua, founded in 1796. Rums are aged in a solera system, unusual for Venezuela, giving a drier style.

Serve: Neat or in a Manhattan twist

Rum & cane · Venezuela

Pampero Aniversario

Toffee, coffee, orange peel and dark sugar, rounded and gently sweet.

Strength: 40% · Age and cask: Aged, oak casks

Pampero has been made since 1938 and the Aniversario, in its leather-pouched bottle, was first released for the brand's anniversary. An easy, widely available Venezuelan ron añejo.

Serve: On the rocks or in a Cuba Libre
