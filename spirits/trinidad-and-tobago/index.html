Trinidad and Tobago: Trinidad rum

Rum & cane · Trinidad and Tobago

Trinidad makes light, elegant column-still rum. Its one big working distillery, home of Angostura bitters, blends and ages rum in Laventille near Port of Spain.

SpiritTrinidad rum
Made fromMolasses
TasteLight and smooth: vanilla, cocoa, orange peel, toffee and gentle oak.
How to drinkNeat, on ice, or in a Queen's Park Swizzle with a good dash of bitters.

How it's made

Molasses is fermented and distilled in multi-column stills to a light, clean spirit, then aged in ex-bourbon barrels in the tropical heat and blended. Caroni, the island's closed heavy-rum distillery, is now a collector's name.

Angostura ferments molasses with its own yeast and distils it in a five-column still, producing several marks from light to heavier, which are aged separately and blended.

History

Sugar cane came to Trinidad in the 18th century and dozens of small distilleries once worked the island. Angostura, founded in Venezuela in 1824 by Dr Johann Siegert to make bitters, moved to Port of Spain in 1875 and began making rum in the 1940s. Caroni, the state distillery, closed in 2002.

Ageing and label terms

Rum ages in ex-bourbon barrels in the hot climate, where it matures quickly with high evaporation. Age statements give the youngest rum in the blend.

Styles

Light white rum, golden blends such as 1919, and older aged rums such as the 7 and 12 Year Old.

Rules and protected names

Rum must be distilled from cane products and aged in Trinidad and Tobago to be sold as Trinidad rum; no single protected geographical indication exists.

How to serve and classic cocktails

The Queen's Park Swizzle (rum, lime, sugar, mint, Angostura bitters) is the island's signature drink; aged rum also suits a rum Old Fashioned.

With food

Doubles, curry goat, pelau and dark chocolate desserts.

Good to know

Caroni bottlings from independent bottlers are now prized and expensive collector's rums.

Producers

  • Angostura
  • Caroni (closed)

Bottles to try

Rum & cane · Trinidad and Tobago

Angostura 1919

Vanilla, caramel, cocoa and toasted coconut, soft and creamy.

Strength: 40% · Age and cask: Blend of rums aged at least 8 years, ex-bourbon barrels

Named after barrels of rum laid down in 1919 that, as the story goes, were rediscovered after a 1932 distillery fire and blended. Made by the House of Angostura, best known for its bitters.

Serve: Neat, on ice, or in a Queen's Park Swizzle

Rum & cane · Trinidad and Tobago

Angostura 7 Year Old

Dark chocolate, molasses, orange and spice, drier than 1919.

Strength: 40% · Age and cask: 7 years, ex-bourbon barrels

A blend of column-still rums aged at least seven years in Trinidad. Good value and a fine introduction to the light, dry Trinidad style.

Serve: In a rum Old Fashioned with Angostura bitters
