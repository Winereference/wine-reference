Elqui Valley: Chilean pisco

Photo: David Vives on Unsplash

Brandy · Chile

Chile's pisco country in the dry north, where high sun and clear skies give very aromatic grapes.

SpiritChilean pisco
Made fromMuscat grapes and Pedro Jiménez
TasteFloral Muscat, peach and citrus.
How to drinkIn a Pisco Sour or with cola (Piscola).

How it's made

Chilean pisco can be diluted to strength and may be aged in wood, giving golden styles alongside clear ones.

Producers

  • Capel
  • Mistral

Bottles to try

Brandy · Chile

Kappa Pisco

Very aromatic: orange blossom, rose, peach, lychee and a clean, dry finish.

Strength: 42.5% · Age and cask: Unaged

Created by the family behind Grand Marnier and Casa Lapostolle, Kappa is made from Muscat grapes grown high in the Elqui Valley. The name comes from the Greek letter for the Southern Cross constellation, which is easy to see in the clear Elqui skies.

Serve: In a Pisco Sour or with tonic
